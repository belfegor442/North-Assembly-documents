1. PURPOSE
============================================================

This document records how North Assembly is currently organized, who holds each formal role, and how responsibilities change when the organization changes.

It is intended to provide a clear operational reference. It does not contain private personnel information.

============================================================
2. AUTHORITY STRUCTURE
============================================================

CEO
|
+-- Director
|
+-- Manager
|
+-- Specialized Teams

The structure above describes the current chain of internal authority. Individual projects may use different working arrangements without changing the formal authority structure unless those changes are officially assigned.

[MANDATORY]
Authority exists only within the role formally assigned to a person.

[MANDATORY]
Delegating a task or responsibility does not automatically transfer the delegator's overall authority to the person receiving it.

============================================================
5. ROLE CHANGES
============================================================

Organizational changes should leave a clear record of who is responsible for what. This is particularly important when a role change also changes access to repositories, systems, projects, or internal information.

[MANDATORY]
Promotions, demotions, transfers, suspensions, and removals must be authorized by the appropriate authority.

[MANDATORY]
Access permissions must reflect the person's current role and actual operational need.

[MANDATORY]
When a role changes, obsolete permissions must be reviewed and removed.
