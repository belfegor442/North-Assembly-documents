PURPOSE

The archive keeps superseded, retired, and historical
documentation that still needs to be preserved for reference and
traceability. It is historical preservation, not a garbage folder.

[MANDATORY]
Archived documents must not be treated as current policy, current
procedure, or current product specifications.

[MANDATORY]
Documents must not be deleted solely because they have been
superseded when historical retention is required.

RETIRED PRODUCTS

Documentation for retired products may remain in 03 - PRODUCTS
with STATUS: ARCHIVED when the record is still useful in place —
DraftPad currently follows that pattern (ADR-0003). Products whose
records are purely historical may be moved here later; a move is
recorded as a document change, not a silent relocation.

LEGACY MATERIAL

Legacy material may be incomplete or no longer reflect current
practices. Its historical status must remain clear so that it is
not mistaken for current documentation.

ARCHIVE RULE

[MANDATORY]
Archived files are preserved as they were. Provenance — where a
file came from, when, and what replaced it — is recorded in index
files such as Superseded/README.txt, not by editing archived
content.

[MANDATORY]
If historical material must be corrected, the reason for the
correction must be clear from the revision history or commit
history.

[MANDATORY]
Nothing is deleted from this archive without an explicit decision
recorded in 01 - CORPORATE/Decisions.

NOT HELD HERE
