USE

Use an appropriate data-protection agreement when the actual relationship and applicable law require one.

MINIMUM SUBJECTS

- subject matter and duration;
- nature and purpose;
- types of personal data;
- categories of data subjects;
- documented instructions;
- confidentiality;
- security;
- subprocessors;
- assistance with rights;
- breach assistance;
- deletion/return;
- audit/compliance information;
- international transfers.

CLASSIFICATION

Do not assume every vendor is a processor. Determine the actual role from the processing arrangement and applicable law.

PUBLIC REPOSITORY

Do not store executed confidential DPAs here. Store only a non-confidential register entry and reference.
