CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION MAY INCLUDE

Unreleased product plans; source code not intended for public release; credentials/secrets; private commercial terms; non-public security information; protected customer information; non-public strategy; restricted third-party information.

NDA DESIGN

Define parties, purpose, confidential information, exclusions, permitted recipients, security duties, compelled disclosure, return/deletion, duration, remedies, governing law and signatures.

RULE

An NDA does not automatically transfer IP, grant employment status, create authority, or authorize processing of personal data beyond the agreed purpose.

PUBLIC REPOSITORY

Never store signed NDAs or private contact information here.
