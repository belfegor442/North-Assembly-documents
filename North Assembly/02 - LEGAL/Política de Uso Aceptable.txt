============================================================
1. PURPOSE
============================================================

This Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) explains how North Assembly products,
applications, websites, services, infrastructure, communication
channels, and other covered resources may be used.

The aim is to protect Users, North Assembly, third parties,
infrastructure, intellectual property, and the availability and
security of North Assembly services.

============================================================
2. SCOPE
============================================================

This Policy applies to Users and other people using a North Assembly
service that refers to this Policy.

It may cover:

* Applications.
* Software.
* Websites.
* Digital services.
* Accounts.
* APIs.
* Custom applications.
* Communication systems.
* North Assembly community spaces.
* Other resources explicitly designated by North Assembly.

The final list of covered services remains pending.

============================================================
3. GENERAL REQUIREMENTS
============================================================

[MANDATORY]
Users must:

* Use North Assembly services lawfully.
* Follow the applicable Terms and Conditions.
* Respect the rights of other Users and third parties.
* Respect security controls.
* Use resources for their intended purposes.
* Provide accurate information where required.
* Follow product-specific rules.

[MANDATORY]
Users must not deliberately misuse North Assembly systems, resources,
accounts, or services.

============================================================
4. UNAUTHORIZED ACCESS
============================================================

[MANDATORY]
Users must not:

* Access another person's account without authorization.
* Obtain another person's credentials without authorization.
* Circumvent authentication.
* Bypass access controls.
* Exploit security restrictions for unauthorized access.
* Access systems or information without permission.
* Keep unauthorized access after discovering it.

[MANDATORY]
Technical knowledge or discovery of a vulnerability does not itself
create permission to access restricted systems.

============================================================
5. SECURITY TESTING AND VULNERABILITIES
============================================================

Security research must remain within the scope of any authorization
that applies.

[MANDATORY]
Users must not knowingly exploit a vulnerability to:

* Obtain unauthorized data.
* Obtain credentials.
* Damage systems.
* Interrupt services.
* Escalate privileges without authorization.
* Establish persistent unauthorized access.
* Harm another User.

[RECOMMENDED]
Security vulnerabilities should be reported through the official
security channel rather than publicly disclosed before North Assembly
has had a reasonable opportunity to respond.

Official security reporting channel:
---

============================================================
6. MALWARE AND MALICIOUS CODE
============================================================

[MANDATORY]
Users must not intentionally use North Assembly services to:

* Distribute malware.
* Deploy ransomware.
* Distribute credential-stealing software.
* Operate malicious payloads.
* Distribute destructive code.
* Create or distribute software intended to compromise systems.
* Conceal malicious activity through North Assembly infrastructure.

This applies whether the target is North Assembly or a third party.

============================================================
7. FRAUD AND DECEPTION
============================================================

[MANDATORY]
Users must not use North Assembly services for:

* Fraud.
* Credential theft.
* Phishing.
* Deliberate impersonation.
* Identity deception.
* Financial scams.
* Social engineering intended to obtain unauthorized access,
  credentials, money, or sensitive information.

[MANDATORY]
Users must not falsely represent themselves as official North Assembly
representatives.

============================================================
8. ABUSE OF INFRASTRUCTURE
============================================================

[MANDATORY]
Users must not intentionally:

* Overload services.
* Consume unreasonable resources through abuse.
* Cause denial of service.
* Interfere with infrastructure.
* Circumvent resource limitations.
* Use automation designed to impair service operation.
* Generate excessive malicious traffic.

Specific resource limits may be established for individual services.

============================================================
9. AUTOMATION AND SCRAPING
============================================================

Automation may be permitted when a service supports it.

[MANDATORY]
Users must not use automation to:

* Circumvent technical restrictions.
* Evade security controls.
* Flood services.
* Harvest restricted information.
* Circumvent rate limits.
* Interfere with normal operation.
* Obtain information they are not authorized to access.

Individual services may define additional API and automation rules.

============================================================
10. PRIVACY AND PERSONAL DATA
============================================================

[MANDATORY]
Users must not intentionally use North Assembly services to collect,
expose, sell, distribute, or otherwise misuse another person's
personal data without appropriate authorization or legal basis.

[MANDATORY]
Users must not publicly expose sensitive information obtained through
North Assembly services for malicious or unauthorized purposes.

[MANDATORY]
Users must respect the Privacy Policy and applicable data-protection
requirements.

============================================================
11. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
============================================================

[MANDATORY]
Users must not knowingly use North Assembly services to infringe the
intellectual property rights of others.

This includes unauthorized use of:

* Copyrighted material.
* Trademarks.
* Software.
* Designs.
* Digital assets.
* Documentation.
* Other protected material.

[MANDATORY]
Applicable licenses must be respected when using third-party software
or content.

============================================================
12. IMPERSONATION AND BRAND ABUSE
============================================================

[MANDATORY]
Users must not:

* Impersonate North Assembly.
* Pretend to be an authorized representative.
* Create deceptive accounts intended to appear official.
* Falsely claim a partnership with North Assembly.
* Misuse North Assembly branding to deceive Users.

[MANDATORY]
Official communications may only be issued through authorized
channels or by authorized representatives.

============================================================
13. HARASSMENT AND ABUSIVE CONDUCT
============================================================

[MANDATORY]
Users must not intentionally use North Assembly services for serious
harassment, threats, intimidation, or targeted abuse.

[MANDATORY]
Threats involving physical harm, credible criminal activity, or
serious safety risks may be escalated through appropriate channels
where permitted or required.

============================================================
14. ILLEGAL USE
============================================================

[MANDATORY]
North Assembly services must not knowingly be used for activities that
violate applicable law.

Users remain responsible for ensuring that their use of North Assembly
services is lawful in their circumstances.

============================================================
15. CUSTOM APPLICATION REQUESTS
============================================================

Custom application requests receive additional review.

[MANDATORY]
Users must not request that North Assembly knowingly create or provide
software intended to facilitate:

* Unauthorized access.
* Malware distribution.
* Credential theft.
* Fraud.
* Destructive attacks.
* Illegal surveillance.
* Abuse of third-party systems.
* Other prohibited activity.

[MANDATORY]
North Assembly may reject requests that create unacceptable legal,
security, operational, or technical risks.

The Custom Applications Policy provides additional requirements.

============================================================
16. CONTENT
============================================================

Where a North Assembly service permits Users to upload or submit
content:

[MANDATORY]
Users must not intentionally submit content for purposes that violate
applicable law or the service's rules.

[MANDATORY]
Users must not knowingly submit malware or malicious executable
content where such content is prohibited.

[MANDATORY]
Submission systems must not be used to attack, test, or disrupt the
service without authorization.

============================================================
17. SECURITY CONTROLS
============================================================

[MANDATORY]
Users must not intentionally circumvent or disable security controls
implemented to protect North Assembly services, accounts, data, or
infrastructure.

This may include:

* Authentication mechanisms.
* Authorization checks.
* Rate limits.
* Access restrictions.
* Integrity checks.
* Update verification.
* Security monitoring controls.

============================================================
18. ACCOUNT ABUSE
============================================================

[MANDATORY]
Users must not create or operate accounts for abusive purposes.

This includes:

* Mass account creation intended to bypass restrictions.
* Fake accounts used for fraud.
* Accounts created to evade enforcement.
* Credential sharing where prohibited.
* Account trading where prohibited.
* Deliberate use of compromised accounts.

============================================================
19. ENFORCEMENT
============================================================

Where legally and contractually permitted, North Assembly may take
appropriate action when this Policy is violated.

Possible actions include:

* Warning.
* Restriction.
* Temporary suspension.
* Removal of specific functionality.
* Account suspension.
* Account termination.
* Removal of prohibited content.
* Blocking abusive activity.
* Referral to another process or authority where appropriate.

[MANDATORY]
Enforcement should be proportionate to the circumstances and
consistent with applicable law and contractual obligations.

============================================================
20. IMMEDIATE SECURITY MEASURES
============================================================

When there is a credible and significant threat to Users,
infrastructure, or service security:

[MANDATORY]
North Assembly may take immediate technical measures reasonably
necessary to contain the threat, including temporary access
restrictions or service isolation, subject to applicable law.

Such measures do not automatically determine whether the User has
violated this Policy.

============================================================
21. APPEALS
============================================================

[PENDING]
The appeal channel does not exist yet. Until it is published, no
suspension or termination under this Policy may be treated as
final.

Where an appeal process exists, it should identify:

* Appeal method.
* Appeal deadline.
* Required information.
* Review process.
* Final decision authority.

[MANDATORY]
A User told of a suspension or restriction must also be told how
to appeal and within what period.

[MANDATORY]
The reviewer must not be the person who made the original
decision, where that is practicable.

[MANDATORY]
A permanent termination must not take effect until an appeal
procedure has been published, unless applicable law permits
immediate action and the User is informed afterwards.

23. EVIDENCE AND INVESTIGATIONS
============================================================

When a violation is reasonably suspected:

[MANDATORY]
North Assembly may review information reasonably necessary to investigate

============================================================
24. THIRD-PARTY SYSTEMS
============================================================

[MANDATORY]
Users must not use North Assembly services to attack, disrupt,
compromise, or gain unauthorized access to third-party systems.

Authorization from a User does not automatically authorize access to
a third-party system.

============================================================
25. RESPONSIBILITY FOR USE
============================================================

Users are responsible for their own activity when using North Assembly
services.

[MANDATORY]
Users must take reasonable steps to protect their accounts and
credentials.

[MANDATORY]
Users must not knowingly allow their account to be used for
prohibited activity.

mechanism where required.
