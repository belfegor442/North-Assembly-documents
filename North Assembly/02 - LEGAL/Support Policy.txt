1. PURPOSE
============================================================

This Policy is intended to define the support North Assembly
provides for its products, once support commitments exist.

[NOTE]
This document is a placeholder. It exists so that the legal set
is complete and so that the absence of a support policy is
visible as a gap rather than as an oversight.

[MANDATORY]
No part of this document may be presented to any person as an
actual support commitment.

============================================================
2. CURRENT STATE
============================================================

[MANDATORY]
No support commitment is currently defined for any North
Assembly product.

The project registry (03 - PRODUCTS/README.txt) does not record
a support channel, availability window, or response time for any
product.

[PENDING]
Whether any product will carry a support commitment at all.
