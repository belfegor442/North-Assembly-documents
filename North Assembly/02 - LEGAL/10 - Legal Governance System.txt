PURPOSE

This document establishes the control system preventing undefined ownership, authority, access, IP provenance, contractual responsibility, data responsibility, succession, and dispute handling.

CORE RULE

If ownership, authority, responsibility, succession, or dispute resolution is not explicitly defined, the matter is UNDEFINED. Undefined matters cannot be treated as settled.

SOURCE-OF-TRUTH RULE

Legal identity -> NA-LGL-002 and the Entity Register.
People/roles -> NA-COR-002.
Document control -> NA-COR-001.
Assets -> NA-COR-011 Asset Register.
IP -> NA-LGL-012 IP Register.
Contracts -> NA-LGL-015 Contract Register.
Vendors/processors -> NA-LGL-014 Processor Register.
Access -> NA-OPS-001 Access Control Policy.
Decisions -> ADR records and Consistency Log.

NON-NEGOTIABLE CONTROLS

1. No material asset without an identified legal owner.
2. No privileged access without an assigned role and recovery route.
3. No production IP without provenance evidence.
4. No material commitment without identified contracting authority.
5. No departure without access revocation and asset recovery.
6. No product launch with unresolved Critical/Blocking legal or security items.
7. No public legal document may contradict the authoritative registers.
8. A technical permission does not itself create legal authority.
9. A repository file does not itself create ownership.
10. Applicable law and executed contracts prevail over internal policy.

LEGAL REVIEW

This system is a governance framework, not legal advice. Documents marked LEGAL REVIEW: REQUIRED must not be represented as legally final until reviewed by a qualified professional for the actual entity, jurisdiction, products, users, data flows and contracts.
