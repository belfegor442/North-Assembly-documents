RULE

The repository master is the controlled source. Public legal pages may only be generated from a version marked eligible for publication.

PUBLICATION GATE

1. Correct legal identity.
2. Correct jurisdiction.
3. Correct contact route.
4. Effective date.
5. Version.
6. Legal review PASSED where required.
7. No unresolved placeholder.
8. Website copy matches approved master.
9. Acceptance/consent mechanism is operational where required.
10. Superseded pages are removed or clearly marked as historical where appropriate.

SYNCHRONIZATION

A public website must never silently diverge from the repository master.

If a divergence is found: record it in the Consistency Log; determine the authoritative version; correct website or repository; record the corrective commit/release.

OLD PUBLIC LEGAL SETS

Historical legal sets remain in ARCHIVE for traceability and must not be presented as current.
