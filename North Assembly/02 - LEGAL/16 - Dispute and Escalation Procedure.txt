TRIGGER

A dispute exists when material parties disagree about ownership, authority, obligations, money, IP, access, data, or interpretation.

PROCEDURE

1. Preserve evidence.
2. Freeze irreversible action on the disputed asset where feasible.
3. Identify the governing document and source of truth.
4. Record the dispute and affected assets.
5. Attempt resolution by the competent internal authority.
6. Escalate to independent/legal review when required.
7. Apply the contractually and legally valid dispute mechanism.
8. Record outcome and corrective action.

NO SELF-HELP

No party may delete, transfer, lock, publish, sell, or materially alter a disputed asset merely to force a resolution, except where necessary for security or legal compliance.

RETALIATION

Good-faith reporting of legal, governance, privacy or security concerns must not itself be treated as misconduct.

EVIDENCE

Preserve relevant contracts, approvals, repository history and access logs subject to applicable law and retention rules.
