1. PURPOSE
============================================================

This Privacy Policy explains how North Assembly may collect, use,
store, disclose, and otherwise process personal data through the
websites, applications, software, services, support channels, and
other products covered by this Policy.

North Assembly intends to handle personal data lawfully, fairly, and
transparently, while applying security measures appropriate to the
nature and risk of the processing.

This Policy should be read together with the applicable Terms and
Conditions, Cookie Policy, and any product-specific privacy notice.

3. APPLICABLE LAW
============================================================

[MANDATORY]
Personal data must be processed in accordance with the data
protection laws applicable to the relevant activity and User.

Where applicable, this may include the General Data Protection
Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and relevant national legislation.

Nothing in this Policy is intended to remove a mandatory right
provided by applicable law.

============================================================
4. PRINCIPLES OF PROCESSING
============================================================

[MANDATORY]
North Assembly will seek to apply these principles when processing
personal data:

* Lawfulness, fairness, and transparency.
* Purpose limitation.
* Data minimization.
* Accuracy.
* Storage limitation.
* Integrity and confidentiality.
* Accountability.

[MANDATORY]
Personal data must not knowingly be processed for a purpose that is
incompatible with the stated or legally permitted purpose.

[MANDATORY]
Only data reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose should be
processed.

============================================================
5. CATEGORIES OF PERSONAL DATA
============================================================

The data processed depends on the service. Depending on the product,
it may include:

* Name or display name.
* Email address and contact information.
* Account information.
* User-provided content.
* Technical and device information.
* IP address.
* Authentication information.
* Security and fraud-prevention information.
* Support communications.
* Error reports.
* Usage information.
* Information submitted through forms or requests.

The actual categories collected by each service must be verified
before publication.

[MANDATORY]
North Assembly must not claim to collect a category of data when the
relevant service does not actually collect it.

============================================================
6. HOW PERSONAL DATA IS COLLECTED
============================================================

Personal data may be obtained:

* Directly from the User.
* Automatically through a website or application.
* Through support or communication channels.
* Through account registration.
* Through custom application requests.
* Through security or operational systems.
* From service providers acting for North Assembly.
* From another lawful source where applicable.

The actual collection methods for each product must be verified
before publication.

============================================================
7. PURPOSES OF PROCESSING
============================================================

Depending on the service, North Assembly may process personal data to:

* Provide requested services.
* Create and manage accounts.
* Respond to support requests.
* Deliver software or digital services.
* Handle custom application requests.
* Maintain service functionality.
* Monitor security and prevent abuse or fraud.
* Detect and investigate security incidents.
* Diagnose technical problems.
* Maintain and improve services.
* Meet legal obligations.
* Establish, exercise, or defend legal claims where permitted.
* Communicate important service information.

[MANDATORY]
Personal data must not be processed for a materially incompatible
purpose without an appropriate legal basis or other lawful
authorization.

============================================================
8. LEGAL BASES
============================================================

Where GDPR or equivalent law applies, each processing activity must
have an appropriate legal basis.

Possible bases include:

* Performance of a contract.
* Compliance with a legal obligation.
* Consent.
* Legitimate interests.
* Protection of vital interests.
* Another legal basis available under applicable law.

The legal basis for each actual processing activity must be
identified before publication.

North Assembly must not describe consent as the legal basis when another
basis actually applies.

============================================================
9. CONSENT
============================================================

Where processing relies on consent:

[MANDATORY]
Consent must be obtained through an appropriate mechanism.

[MANDATORY]
Users must be able to withdraw consent where applicable.

[MANDATORY]
Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing that occurred
before withdrawal.

The actual consent mechanisms used by North Assembly must be documented.

============================================================
10. LEGITIMATE INTERESTS
============================================================

Where North Assembly relies on legitimate interests, the relevant
processing must be assessed and balanced against the rights and
interests of affected individuals.

The legitimate interests relied upon for each relevant activity must
be documented before publication.

============================================================
11. DATA MINIMIZATION
============================================================

[MANDATORY]
North Assembly must limit personal-data collection and processing to
what is necessary for the relevant purpose.

[MANDATORY]
Personal data must not be collected merely because it is technically
available.

Privacy by default means that initial processing settings should
limit personal data to what is necessary for the defined purpose.

============================================================
12. ACCURACY
============================================================

[MANDATORY]
North Assembly must take reasonable measures to keep personal data
accurate when accuracy matters to the processing.

[MANDATORY]
Users may request correction of inaccurate personal data where
applicable.

============================================================
13. DATA RETENTION
============================================================

Personal data must not be retained indefinitely without an
appropriate reason.

Retention periods or criteria must be defined for relevant categories
of data before publication.

Retention may depend on:

* The purpose of collection.
* Legal obligations.
* Security requirements.
* Account status.
* Disputes or legal claims.
* Technical requirements.

============================================================
14. RECIPIENTS OF PERSONAL DATA
============================================================

Depending on the service, personal data may be processed by:

* Authorized North Assembly personnel.
* Hosting providers.
* Infrastructure providers.
* Software providers.
* Communication providers.
* Security providers.
* Analytics providers, where used.
* Professional advisers where necessary.
* Public authorities where legally required.

The actual recipients must be identified before publication.

[MANDATORY]
Personal data must not be disclosed merely because doing so is
technically convenient.

============================================================
15. DATA PROCESSORS
============================================================

When a third party processes personal data on behalf of North Assembly,
the relationship must be assessed and managed according to
applicable law.

Where relevant, North Assembly must document:

* Processor identity.
* Processing purpose.
* Categories of data.
* Contract or other legal arrangement.
* Sub-processors.
* Security requirements.
* Data location.

Where GDPR applies, processors must provide sufficient guarantees
for appropriate technical and organizational measures.

============================================================
16. INTERNATIONAL DATA TRANSFERS
============================================================

Personal data may be processed or transferred outside the User's
country or relevant jurisdiction when permitted by applicable law.

Before publication, North Assembly must identify, where applicable:

* Whether international transfers occur.
* Relevant countries.
* Transfer mechanism.
* Safeguards.
* Relevant providers.

Transfers must comply with applicable data-protection requirements.

============================================================
17. SECURITY
============================================================

[MANDATORY]
North Assembly must use technical and organizational measures
appropriate to the risks of its personal-data processing.

Depending on the service, measures may include:

* Access controls.
* Authentication controls.
* Encryption where appropriate.
* Credential protection.
* Monitoring.
* Backups.
* Vulnerability management.
* Incident response.
* Access limitation.

[MANDATORY]
Access to personal data must be limited according to legitimate
operational need.

============================================================
18. DATA BREACHES AND SECURITY INCIDENTS
============================================================

A personal-data breach may involve unauthorized access,
disclosure, alteration, destruction, or loss of personal data.

[MANDATORY]
Suspected breaches must be assessed and handled according to the
notification and communication duties imposed by applicable law.

[MANDATORY]
Security incidents involving personal data must be escalated through
the appropriate internal process.

============================================================
19. USER RIGHTS
============================================================

Depending on applicable law, individuals may have rights including:

* Access.
* Rectification.
* Erasure.
* Restriction of processing.
* Objection.
* Data portability.
* Withdrawal of consent where consent is the legal basis.
* Rights concerning automated decision-making and profiling where
  applicable.
* The right to complain to a competent supervisory authority.

The availability and scope of these rights depend on the applicable
law and circumstances.

============================================================

Any continued retention remains subject to applicable law and the
relevant retention requirements.
