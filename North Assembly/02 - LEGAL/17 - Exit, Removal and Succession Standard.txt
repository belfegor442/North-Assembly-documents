TRIGGERS

RESIGNATION | TERMINATION | ROLE CHANGE | SUSPENSION | COMPROMISE | INACTIVITY | DEATH/INCAPACITY

IMMEDIATE ACTIONS

- revoke or reduce privileged access;
- rotate secrets where appropriate;
- transfer organization-owned assets;
- review pending approvals;
- preserve records;
- update NA-COR-002;
- update access and asset registers.

CRITICAL ASSET REQUIREMENT

Every critical domain, repository, cloud account, payment system, identity provider, signing key and production database must have an organization-controlled ownership/recovery route and a backup custodian.

SUCCESSION

A role may be vacant. A vacancy grants no authority and no access.

LEGAL EFFECT

Removal from an operational role does not by itself determine employment status, contractual rights, corporate office, or IP ownership. Those matters are governed by the applicable agreement and law.
