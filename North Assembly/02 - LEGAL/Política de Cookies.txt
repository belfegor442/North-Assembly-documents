============================================================
1. PURPOSE
============================================================

This Cookie Policy explains how North Assembly may use cookies and
similar technologies when Users access websites, applications, or
online services covered by this Policy.

It explains what these technologies are used for, how they may be
classified, and what choices Users may have when consent is
required.

This Policy should be read together with the North Assembly Privacy
Policy and Terms and Conditions.

============================================================
2. WHAT ARE COOKIES
============================================================

Cookies are small files, identifiers, or similar technologies that
may be stored on a User's device when a website or service is used.

They may be used to:

* Keep a service working.
* Maintain sessions.
* Remember preferences.
* Support security features.
* Measure usage.
* Understand technical problems.
* Improve services.
* Provide other necessary functionality.

Not every North Assembly service uses cookies or similar technologies.

============================================================
3. SCOPE
============================================================

This Policy applies only to North Assembly websites, applications, and
services that use cookies or similar technologies and refer to this
Policy.

The list of covered services remains pending until the relevant
services are confirmed.

============================================================
4. TYPES OF TECHNOLOGIES
============================================================

Depending on the service, North Assembly may use:

* HTTP cookies.
* Local storage.
* Session storage.
* Similar browser storage mechanisms.
* Other technologies with comparable functionality.

The actual technologies used by each service must be identified
before this Policy is published as final.

============================================================
5. COOKIE CATEGORIES
============================================================

Cookies and similar technologies may be grouped according to their
purpose.

5.1 STRICTLY NECESSARY

These technologies support functions that are essential for the
service, such as:

* Authentication.
* Session management.
* Security.
* Load balancing.
* Basic technical functionality.
* Essential service preferences.

[MANDATORY]
Technologies classified as strictly necessary must only be used for
purposes compatible with their essential function.

The actual necessary technologies used by North Assembly remain to be
identified for each service.

5.2 PREFERENCES

Preference technologies may remember choices such as language,
interface settings, or other configuration preferences.

The actual preference technologies used by North Assembly remain to be
identified for each service.

5.3 ANALYTICS

Analytics technologies may help North Assembly understand how a service
is used and identify technical or usability problems.

They may measure things such as:

* Page or feature usage.
* Performance.
* Errors.
* General traffic patterns.

The providers and technologies actually used by North Assembly remain
to be identified before publication.

5.4 ADVERTISING OR MARKETING

If North Assembly uses advertising, marketing, profiling, or
cross-service tracking technologies, they must be documented
separately with their purpose, provider, legal basis, and applicable
consent requirements.

============================================================
6. NECESSARY TECHNOLOGIES AND CONSENT
============================================================

Depending on applicable law, some technologies may be used without
consent when they are genuinely necessary for the service.

[MANDATORY]
North Assembly must not describe a technology as strictly necessary
merely to avoid obtaining consent when consent is legally required.

The classification of each technology must be verified before final
publication.

============================================================
7. CONSENT FOR NON-NECESSARY TECHNOLOGIES
============================================================

Where consent is legally required:

[MANDATORY]
Non-essential cookies and similar technologies must not be activated
before valid consent has been obtained.

[MANDATORY]
Users must receive appropriate information before or when consent is
requested.

[MANDATORY]
Users must be able to withdraw consent through an appropriate
mechanism where the law requires this.

[MANDATORY]
Withdrawing consent must not be made unreasonably difficult.

============================================================
8. COOKIE CONSENT INTERFACE
============================================================

Where a consent mechanism is required, the interface should explain
the available choices clearly.

Before publication, North Assembly must determine the applicable:

* Consent banner.
* Preference center.
* Cookie categories.
* Accept mechanism.
* Reject mechanism.
* Settings mechanism.
* Withdrawal mechanism.
* Consent-record mechanism.

[RECOMMENDED]
The interface should make acceptance and rejection choices clear and
should not use manipulative design intended to influence the User's
decision unfairly.

============================================================
9. CONSENT RECORDS
============================================================

Where consent is required:

[MANDATORY]
North Assembly must keep appropriate evidence of consent in accordance
with applicable legal requirements.

The final implementation must identify:

* What consent information is stored.
* Where it is stored.
* How long it is retained.
* How changes to consent are recorded.
* How withdrawal is recorded.

============================================================
10. THIRD-PARTY COOKIES
============================================================

Some services may use technologies provided by third parties, such
as:

* Analytics providers.
* Embedded-content providers.
* Security providers.
* Authentication providers.
* Advertising providers.

The actual third parties used by each service must be identified.

[MANDATORY]
North Assembly must not describe a third-party technology as first-party
when it is operated by another party.

============================================================
11. INTERNATIONAL PROCESSING
============================================================

North Assembly must determine whether any cookie provider or related
service transfers or processes personal data outside the relevant
jurisdiction.

Where applicable, the Privacy Policy must explain the relevant
processing and safeguards.

13. RETENTION AND DURATION
============================================================

Cookie duration depends on the technology and its purpose.

Possible categories include:

* Session-based.
* Short-term.
* Long-term.
* Persistent.

The actual duration of each technology must be documented before
final publication.

============================================================
14. PERSONAL DATA
============================================================

Cookies and similar technologies may involve personal data or
information that can be linked to an identifiable individual.

[MANDATORY]
When cookie-related processing involves personal data, it must also
comply with the Privacy Policy and applicable data-protection
requirements.

[MANDATORY]
North Assembly must not describe information as anonymous when it can
actually be linked to an identifiable individual, unless the claim is
appropriately qualified.

============================================================
15. BROWSER CONTROLS
============================================================

Users may have browser-level controls for managing cookies. The
available controls depend on the browser and device being used.

[RECOMMENDED]
North Assembly may provide general guidance explaining how Users can
manage cookies in common browsers.

============================================================
16. CHANGING COOKIE PREFERENCES
============================================================

Where North Assembly provides a consent-management mechanism:

[MANDATORY]
Users must be able to access the relevant preference controls again
where required by applicable law.

The final publication must identify where those controls can be
found and how they can be accessed.

============================================================
17. COOKIE POLICY UPDATES
============================================================

North Assembly may update this Policy when its technologies, services,
legal obligations, or processing practices change.

[MANDATORY]
The current version and effective date must be identifiable.

[MANDATORY]
Where a material change affects User rights or consent, North Assembly
must provide the appropriate notice or consent mechanism where
required by law.

============================================================
18. RELATIONSHIP WITH PRIVACY POLICY
============================================================

This Policy focuses on cookies and similar technologies.

The Privacy Policy provides the broader information about the
processing of personal data.

[MANDATORY]
When a cookie or similar technology processes personal data, the
relevant information must remain consistent across both documents.

============================================================
19. SECURITY
============================================================

[MANDATORY]
North Assembly must take reasonable measures to prevent unauthorized
access to information associated with its cookie and tracking
systems.

[MANDATORY]
Cookie technologies must not intentionally be configured in a way
that unnecessarily exposes sensitive information.

[RECOMMENDED]
Security-sensitive cookies should use appropriate security
attributes and configuration supported by the relevant platform.

============================================================

functions similar to cookies, including:

* Tracking pixels.
* Web beacons.
* Local storage.
* Similar identifiers.
* Embedded tracking technologies.

North Assembly must verify whether any such technologies are currently
used before final publication.
