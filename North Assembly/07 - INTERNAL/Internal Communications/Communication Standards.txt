NORTH ASSEMBLY
INTERNAL COMMUNICATION STANDARDS

Version: 1.1
Status: IN FORCE
Classification: INTERNAL

PURPOSE

Internal communication should make it possible for the team to share information, make decisions, and coordinate work without exposing information to people who are not supposed to receive it.

[MANDATORY]
Internal communication must remain within authorized channels for the information's classification.

[MANDATORY]
Restricted and confidential information must only be shared with authorized recipients.

[MANDATORY]
Operational decisions affecting products, security, access, or releases must be communicated to the people responsible for acting on them.

[RECOMMENDED]
Important decisions should be recorded so that their reasoning and outcome remain traceable later.

[MANDATORY]
Internal communication must not be used to impersonate another member or authority.

[PENDING]
Formal communication-channel matrix.
[PENDING]
Formal escalation matrix.
