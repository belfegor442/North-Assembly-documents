PURPOSE

These rules set the basic expectations for people working with North
Assembly. They are intended to keep responsibilities clear, protect
the company and its work, and prevent avoidable problems between
team members.

1. AUTHORITY AND RESPONSIBILITY

[MANDATORY]
Team members must work within their assigned authority and responsibilities. A person's role does not give them automatic authority over areas outside that role.

[MANDATORY]
A role title is an operational label. It must never be presented as a legal or corporate office.

2. ROLES AND VACANCIES

The list of roles, their holders and any vacancies is recorded in
NA-COR-002. Recruitment and the terminology for working
relationships are governed by NA-COR-004.

[MANDATORY]
A vacant position belongs to no one. No person may act for,
represent, or exercise the access of a position that is vacant.

[MANDATORY]
A person must not be given a role that has no identified work
behind it.

[MANDATORY]
Different working relationships are not interchangeable. Where the
exact relationship is unknown, use "collaborator" or "member of
the project".

3. INFORMATION AND SECURITY

[MANDATORY]
Members must protect internal, restricted, and confidential information from unauthorized access or disclosure.

[MANDATORY]
This repository is public. Documents stored here must not contain
secrets, credentials, personal contact data, private personnel
records, or non-public infrastructure detail. See ADR-0006.

[MANDATORY]
Members must not intentionally interfere with another member's authorized work, accounts, repositories, or infrastructure.

[MANDATORY]
Security incidents and significant operational problems must be escalated through the appropriate channel.

4. ROLE CHANGES AND DEPARTURES

[MANDATORY]
When a member leaves a role or leaves North Assembly, applicable access must be revoked.

[MANDATORY]
When a role changes or becomes vacant, NA-COR-002 must be updated
in the same change cycle.

[RECOMMENDED]
Important work should be documented well enough to allow responsibilities to be transferred when necessary.
