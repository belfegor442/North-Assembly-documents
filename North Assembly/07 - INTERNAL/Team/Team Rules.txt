NORTH ASSEMBLY
TEAM RULES

Version: 1.1
Status: IN FORCE
Classification: INTERNAL

PURPOSE

These rules set the basic expectations for people working with North Assembly. They are intended to keep responsibilities clear, protect the company and its work, and prevent avoidable problems between team members.

1. AUTHORITY AND RESPONSIBILITY

[MANDATORY]
Team members must work within their assigned authority and responsibilities. A person's role does not give them automatic authority over areas outside that role.

2. INFORMATION AND SECURITY

[MANDATORY]
Members must protect internal, restricted, and confidential information from unauthorized access or disclosure.

[MANDATORY]
Members must not intentionally interfere with another member's authorized work, accounts, repositories, or infrastructure.

[MANDATORY]
Security incidents and significant operational problems must be escalated through the appropriate channel.

3. ROLE CHANGES AND DEPARTURES

[MANDATORY]
When a member leaves a role or leaves North Assembly, applicable access must be revoked.

[RECOMMENDED]
Important work should be documented well enough to allow responsibilities to be transferred when necessary.

END OF DOCUMENT
