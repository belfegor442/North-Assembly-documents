NORTH ASSEMBLY
MEETING STANDARDS

Version: 1.1
Status: IN FORCE
Classification: INTERNAL

PURPOSE

Meetings should have a clear reason to exist. They are useful for making decisions, coordinating work, resolving issues, or transferring information that is easier to discuss than to communicate asynchronously.

1. REQUIREMENTS

[RECOMMENDED]
Meetings should have a defined purpose and include the people whose participation is actually relevant.

[RECOMMENDED]
Important decisions should be recorded together with the decision itself, the responsible person, and any relevant follow-up.

[MANDATORY]
Restricted information must only be discussed with authorized participants.

[MANDATORY]
Meeting records must be stored according to their information classification.

[PENDING]
Formal meeting-record template.
[PENDING]
Formal retention period.

END OF DOCUMENT
