STATE OF THE BRAND RECORD

The brand documentation is honest but incomplete. The following
are explicitly pending and must not be assumed:

* Formal color palette.
* Formal typography specification.
* Logo spacing and minimum-size rules.
* The authoritative location of current logo and banner assets.

Legacy material from the previous identity is preserved under
08 - ARCHIVE/Legacy Brand and is not part of the current brand
(ADR-0001).

USE THIS SECTION FOR

- Brand identity and naming rules.
- Visual consistency.
- Marketing standards.
- Public-facing brand use.

DO NOT USE THIS SECTION FOR

- Legal trademark status: no registration is documented. See
  NA-LGL-006 and NA-BRD-001 §1.
- Website content rules: see 06 - PUBLIC/Website.
