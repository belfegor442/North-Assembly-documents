NORTH ASSEMBLY
BRAND GUIDELINES

Version: 1.1
Status: IN FORCE
Effective Date: September 12, 2026
Owner: North Assembly
Classification: INTERNAL

1. BRAND IDENTITY

North Assembly is an independent software studio. The brand should feel like a real small studio building real software, not like a generic corporate template.

[MANDATORY]
The North Assembly name and logo must not be altered in a way that falsely suggests an official communication or product.

2. VOICE

North Assembly should communicate clearly and directly. Technical subjects can be technical, but they should still sound like they were written by people who actually work on the projects.

Avoid empty corporate language, exaggerated claims, and descriptions that make unfinished work sound finished.

[MANDATORY]
Marketing must not knowingly make false claims about products, security, performance, availability, or capabilities.

[RECOMMENDED]
Prefer specific statements over vague claims. Say what a product does, what it does not do, and what stage it is actually in.

3. VISUAL ASSETS

[MANDATORY]
Use approved logos and assets when an official North Assembly identity is required.

[MANDATORY]
Third-party assets must be used according to their applicable licenses.

[PENDING]
Formal color palette.

[PENDING]
Formal typography specification.

[PENDING]
Logo spacing and minimum-size rules.
