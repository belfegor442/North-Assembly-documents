PURPOSE

These standards cover public promotional communication made on behalf of North Assembly. The basic expectation is simple: marketing should describe the company and its products accurately, without turning unfinished work or uncertain claims into something that sounds guaranteed.

1. ACCURACY

[MANDATORY]
Marketing must be accurate and must not knowingly misrepresent product capabilities, availability, security, pricing, status, or ownership.

[MANDATORY]
Experimental, beta, preview, and development products must be identified accurately whenever their status matters to the communication.

[MANDATORY]
Third-party trademarks and assets must be used according to applicable rights and licenses.

2. CLAIMS

[MANDATORY]
Performance or security claims must have a reasonable factual basis.

[MANDATORY]
Testimonials, comparisons, and endorsements must not be fabricated or presented as genuine when they are not.

[RECOMMENDED]
Prefer specific claims that can be explained or supported over vague statements about being better, faster, safer, or more advanced.

3. PUBLICATION

[RECOMMENDED]
Material announcements should be reviewed before publication, particularly when they describe product status, security, pricing, or other information that could materially affect users.
