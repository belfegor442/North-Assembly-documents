NORTH ASSEMBLY
INCIDENT RESPONSE

Version: 1.1
Status: IN FORCE
Effective Date: September 12, 2026
Owner: North Assembly
Classification: RESTRICTED

PURPOSE

This procedure defines the basic response expected when North Assembly experiences a security incident or a major operational incident.

The priority is to limit harm first, restore safe operation, and then understand what happened well enough to prevent the same problem from repeating.

1. INCIDENT TYPES

An incident may affect:

* Confidentiality.
* Integrity.
* Availability.
* Authenticity.
* User security.
* Organizational infrastructure.

2. RESPONSE ORDER

1. DETECT
2. CONTAIN
3. PROTECT
4. RECOVER
5. INVESTIGATE
6. CORRECT
7. PREVENT RECURRENCE

The exact actions may vary with the incident, but containment and protection should not be delayed unnecessarily while waiting for a complete investigation.

3. MANDATORY ACTIONS

[MANDATORY]
Significant incidents must be reported to the appropriate responsible person as soon as reasonably possible.

[MANDATORY]
Known compromised credentials must be secured.

[MANDATORY]
Evidence relevant to understanding the incident must not be intentionally destroyed.

[MANDATORY]
Recovery actions must not knowingly create an additional unacceptable security risk.

4. POST-INCIDENT REVIEW

[RECOMMENDED]
Significant incidents should receive a post-incident review once the immediate situation is under control.

The review should identify:

* Root or contributing causes.
* Impact.
* Actions taken.
* Recovery status.
* Corrective actions.
* Preventive actions.

[PENDING]
Formal incident severity matrix.
[PENDING]
Formal incident reporting channel.
[PENDING]
Evidence-retention requirements.
