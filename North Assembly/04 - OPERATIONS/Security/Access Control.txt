PURPOSE

Access to North Assembly systems and information should follow a simple rule: people receive the permissions they need for their work, and no more than that without a clear reason.

1. PRINCIPLES

[MANDATORY]
Access must be based on operational need.

[MANDATORY]
Permissions must be limited to what is reasonably necessary for the person's responsibilities.

[MANDATORY]
Credentials must not be shared.

[MANDATORY]
Access must be revoked when the operational need ends.

2. ROLE-BASED ACCESS

Access should match the person's actual role and assigned responsibilities rather than a generic assumption about their position.

[MANDATORY]
A role change requires an access review so that permissions can be added, removed, or adjusted as appropriate.

[MANDATORY]
Termination or removal from a relevant responsibility requires prompt revocation of applicable access.

3. ADMINISTRATIVE ACCESS

[MANDATORY]
Administrative access must receive stronger protection than ordinary access.

[RECOMMENDED]
Multi-factor authentication should be enabled on critical accounts whenever available.

[RECOMMENDED]
Critical permissions should be reviewed periodically.

4. CREDENTIAL INCIDENTS

[MANDATORY]
Suspected credential compromise must be reported and the affected credential revoked, rotated, or otherwise secured as soon as reasonably possible.
