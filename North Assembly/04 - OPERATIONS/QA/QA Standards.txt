PURPOSE

QA exists to check that releases meet their intended functional, security, stability, and compatibility requirements. Testing should provide useful evidence about whether a release is ready, not simply produce a checkbox.

1. TESTING

[MANDATORY]
Stable releases must undergo testing appropriate to their functionality and risk.

[MANDATORY]
Known critical defects must be evaluated before a stable release.

[RECOMMENDED]
Major changes should receive regression testing so that existing functionality is not broken unintentionally.

[RECOMMENDED]
Important fixes should retain enough evidence to show that the fix was actually tested.

2. BUG REPORTING

[MANDATORY]
Important bugs must contain enough information to determine their impact and, where possible, reproduce the issue.

A useful bug report should identify:

* Affected product and version.
* Expected behavior.
* Actual behavior.
* Reproduction steps.
* Relevant logs or other evidence.
* Severity or practical impact.

3. RELEASE GATES

[MANDATORY]
A release must not be marked stable simply because development is complete. Its required validation must also be considered complete or explicitly excepted.

[MANDATORY]
Known unresolved critical issues must either block the release or be explicitly accepted by the responsible authority based on the project's risk.

[PENDING]
Formal severity matrix.
[PENDING]
Formal test-case management system.
