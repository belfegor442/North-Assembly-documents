PURPOSE

This document defines the basic lifecycle for software defects and operational issues. The goal is to make problems traceable from the first report through verification and closure.

1. LIFECYCLE

REPORTED -> TRIAGED -> ASSIGNED -> INVESTIGATING -> FIXED -> VERIFIED -> CLOSED

An issue may instead be rejected, marked as a duplicate, deferred, or reopened when the situation requires it. The history of the issue should remain understandable when its state changes.

2. REPORTING

[MANDATORY]
Important defects must be recorded in an authorized tracking system.

[MANDATORY]
A report must contain enough information to assess its impact and reproduce the issue where possible.

3. TRIAGE

Issues should be evaluated using the factors that matter to the actual situation, including:

* Security impact.
* Data integrity impact.
* Availability impact.
* User impact.
* Reproducibility.
* Scope.

4. SEVERITY

CRITICAL
Immediate or potentially severe impact to security, data integrity, or essential operation.

HIGH
Major functional, security, or reliability impact.

MEDIUM
Meaningful defect with limited scope or an available workaround.

LOW
Minor defect with limited operational impact.

INFORMATIONAL
Observation, suggestion, or improvement that does not represent a material defect.

5. CLOSURE

[MANDATORY]
A defect marked as fixed must be verified before closure when verification is technically possible.

[MANDATORY]
Reopened issues must retain their previous history rather than being treated as new issues without context.
