NORTH ASSEMBLY
INFRASTRUCTURE STANDARDS

Version: 1.1
Status: IN FORCE
Effective Date: September 12, 2026
Owner: North Assembly
Classification: INTERNAL

PURPOSE

These standards set the basic expectations for the infrastructure North Assembly relies on: repositories, domains, DNS, email, communication services, build systems, hosting, storage, servers, and related services.

The goal is straightforward: infrastructure should be accessible to the people who need it, protected from unnecessary access, and changed in a way that can be understood and recovered when something goes wrong.

1. ACCESS

[MANDATORY]
Infrastructure access must be granted according to actual operational need. Having a role in the company does not automatically mean having administrative access to every service.

[MANDATORY]
Administrative credentials must not be shared between people.

[MANDATORY]
Access must be removed when the reason for that access ends, including when a person leaves a project or the company.

[RECOMMENDED]
Critical infrastructure should use multi-factor authentication whenever the service supports it.

2. CHANGES

[MANDATORY]
Material infrastructure changes must be traceable. The team should be able to identify what changed, who made the change, and why it was made.

[MANDATORY]
Changes to production infrastructure must be performed by authorized personnel.

[RECOMMENDED]
High-risk changes should have a rollback or recovery plan before they are applied.

3. SECRETS

[MANDATORY]
API keys, passwords, access tokens, private keys, and similar secrets must be kept using appropriate secret-management controls. They must not be placed in source code or public repositories.

[MANDATORY]
Credentials that are known or reasonably suspected to be compromised must be revoked or rotated as soon as reasonably possible.

4. RECOVERY

[MANDATORY]
Critical information must have a reasonable recovery mechanism where technically possible.

[PENDING]
Formal production architecture.
[PENDING]
Approved hosting providers.
[PENDING]
Infrastructure inventory.
[PENDING]
Backup schedule and retention requirements.
[PENDING]
Formal RPO/RTO requirements.

5. RESPONSIBILITY

[MANDATORY]
Infrastructure owners must keep access and configuration information reasonably current.

[RECOMMENDED]
Important infrastructure decisions should be documented in a way that another authorized team member can understand without relying entirely on personal knowledge.

END OF DOCUMENT
