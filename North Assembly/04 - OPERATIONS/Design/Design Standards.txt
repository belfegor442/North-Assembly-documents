1. PURPOSE

These standards set the baseline for how North Assembly designs products, interfaces, and communication materials. The goal is not to make everything look identical, but to make sure design decisions are deliberate, usable, honest, and consistent with the company’s identity.

2. DESIGN PRINCIPLES

[MANDATORY]
Every design must have a clear purpose. It must support a product, communication, usability, or other defined objective.

[MANDATORY]
Design must not deliberately suggest capabilities, features, compatibility, performance, or status that a product does not actually have.

[MANDATORY]
Official brand assets must follow the approved North Assembly Brand Guidelines.

[RECOMMENDED]
Interfaces should favor readability, consistency, accessibility, and predictable interaction over decoration that does not serve a purpose.

3. PRODUCT UI

[MANDATORY]
Important actions must have clearly distinguishable states and provide appropriate feedback when the user interacts with them.

[MANDATORY]
Error states must explain the problem clearly enough for the user to understand what happened and, where appropriate, what can be done next. Error messages must not expose passwords, keys, tokens, internal credentials, or other sensitive information.

[RECOMMENDED]
Repeated interface patterns should behave consistently throughout the same product. If a pattern needs to work differently, the difference should have a clear reason.

4. ASSETS

[MANDATORY]
Third-party assets may only be used in accordance with their applicable licenses and usage conditions.

[MANDATORY]
Final design assets must be stored in an authorized repository or asset-management system so the team can identify and maintain the current version.

[PENDING]
Formal asset naming convention.
[PENDING]
Formal design review workflow.
