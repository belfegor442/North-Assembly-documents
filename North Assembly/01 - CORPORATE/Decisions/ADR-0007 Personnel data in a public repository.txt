ADR-0007
PERSONNEL DATA IN A PUBLIC REPOSITORY

Date: October 1, 2026
Status: ACCEPTED
Classification: INTERNAL
Owner: North Assembly
Related: NA-COR-001, NA-COR-002, ADR-0002, ADR-0006

CONTEXT

This repository is public on GitHub (ADR-0006). During the
October 2026 legal review it was found that several documents
recorded identifiable former members together with statements
about their work:

  * ADR-0002 named five former members and described the output of
    the positions they had held.
  * The Consistency Log restated the former roster.
  * Archived roster documents listed former members by name.

Those records are personal data. Published in a world-readable
repository they have no legal basis, no way for the person to
object, and no operational purpose. Two of this repository's own
rules already prohibit them: NA-COR-001 forbids private personnel
records in INTERNAL documents, and ADR-0006 assumes the repository
holds no such records.

PROBLEM

The organization's own decision records contradicted its own
data-handling rules, and the contradiction was public.

DECISION

1. No document in this repository records performance,
   disciplinary, or evaluative statements about an identifiable
   individual.

2. Former members are referred to by role only. Statements about
   the previous structure are made about positions, not people.

3. Current members may be listed by their public identifier in
   NA-COR-002 (Organization), which is the single place where
   holders and vacancies are recorded. No other document
   enumerates them.

4. Roster material in 08 - ARCHIVE is redacted in place. The
   redaction uses a visible marker so a reader can tell that
   something was removed, and the reason is recorded in
   Superseded/README.txt. This is a deliberate, recorded exception
   to the archive rule that archived files are preserved as they
   were: personal-data protection takes precedence over byte
   fidelity.

5. A repository-wide check for former-member identifiers is part
   of any documentation audit (NA-COR-001 §10).

CONSEQUENCES

* ADR-0002 was rewritten before this repository was published in
  its reformed state.
* Archived rosters keep their structure and roles; names are
  replaced with a redaction marker.
* Identifiers committed to git history before October 1, 2026 are
  not removed by this decision. Removing them requires rewriting
  repository history and force-pushing, which is destructive and
  affects forks, clones and open links. Owner decision
  2026-10-03: history is left as committed; the identifiers remain
  readable in history and the exposure is accepted. Reopened only
  by a new explicit owner instruction.

ALTERNATIVES CONSIDERED

* Keep the records as historical fact - rejected: personal data
  without purpose or legal basis in a public repository.
* Make the repository private - rejected: decided against in
  ADR-0006; the documentation system is meant to be the public
  reference.
* Delete the affected documents entirely - rejected: the decision
  history and the archive have legitimate traceability value.
