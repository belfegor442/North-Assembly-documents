PURPOSE

This directory records decisions that change how North Assembly
is organized, documented, or positioned — decisions that would
otherwise be invisible once the documents they affected have been
edited.

[MANDATORY]
A material organizational, product, legal, or structural decision
is recorded here, not only as an edit to an existing document.

[MANDATORY]
A decision record is never rewritten to fit later facts. A
reversal gets a new ADR that references the old one.

  Date / Status / Classification / Owner / Related

  CONSEQUENCES -> ALTERNATIVES CONSIDERED -> RELATED DOCUMENTS
