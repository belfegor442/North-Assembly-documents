ADR-0003
DRAFTPAD RETIREMENT

Date: September 12, 2026
Status: ACCEPTED
Classification: INTERNAL
Owner: North Assembly
Related: NA-PRD-DRA-001, NA-PRD-DRA-002, 08 - ARCHIVE

CONTEXT

DraftPad was part of the North Assembly product set alongside
Monix, Datalog, CryptCast and Telenet.

PROBLEM

The project is no longer part of active development, but its
existence and history remain relevant.

DECISION

DraftPad is RETIRED. Its documentation is retained as a
historical record, marked STATUS: ARCHIVED, and it must not be
described as active or supported.

CONSEQUENCES

* The product registry records DraftPad as ARCHIVED.
* The Manual Corporativo must not list DraftPad among current
  identified projects without its retired status.
* Public mentions must make availability and support status
  clear.
* If the lifecycle status ever changes, the product documents and
  this record must both be updated.
