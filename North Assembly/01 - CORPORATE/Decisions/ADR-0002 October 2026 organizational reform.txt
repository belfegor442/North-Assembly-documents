ADR-0002
OCTOBER 2026 ORGANIZATIONAL REFORM

Date: October 1, 2026
Status: ACCEPTED
Classification: INTERNAL
Owner: North Assembly
Related: NA-COR-002, NA-COR-004, NA-COR-003, ADR-0007

CONTEXT

The previous organizational structure was:

  CEO        belfegor442
  Director   Anti
  Manager    held by a former member
  Designer   two positions, held by former members
  Tester     held by a former member
  Developer  previously held by a former member

[MANDATORY]
Former members are referred to by role only. This repository must
not record the identifiers or performance assessments of
identifiable individuals (ADR-0007).

The structure produced a mismatch between positions and productive
work: the CEO carried a very large number of active projects, one
position produced no measurable operational output, one position
had no assigned deliverable, and two positions produced no
completed work.

This is recorded as an organizational problem. It is not an
evaluation of any person, and no such evaluation is recorded in
this repository.

PROBLEM

The organization needed a structure that reflects actual work
rather than an intended chart, without losing the ability to grow.

DECISION

1. The structure becomes:

     CEO      belfegor442
     Director Anti
     Manager  Doughtking12
     Developer   VACANT (intentional)
     Designer    VACANT (intentional)

2. Doughtking12 replaces the previous Manager.

3. Developer and Designer remain deliberately unfilled.

4. Vacancy becomes a documented, legitimate state rather than a
   gap to be filled (NA-COR-002 §4).

5. Recruitment is governed by a fixed pipeline:
   need -> work -> role -> person (NA-COR-004).

RATIONALE

Positions must be justified by identified work. Creating roles to
complete a chart produces activity without deliverables.

CONSEQUENCES

* NA-COR-002 records holders and vacancies as the single source
  of truth.
* The Manual Corporativo references NA-COR-002 instead of
  restating the roster.
* Public "people" pages that still show the previous roster must
  be corrected (external website track).
* Projects requiring architecture, core engineering, or internal
  infrastructure remain with the CEO and Director by default.

ALTERNATIVES CONSIDERED

* Fill Developer and Designer immediately — rejected: no
  identified work justifies either position right now.
* Remove the positions entirely — rejected: the work will
  reappear; keeping them vacant documents the intent.
