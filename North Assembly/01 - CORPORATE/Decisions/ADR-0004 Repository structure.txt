ADR-0004
DOCUMENTATION REPOSITORY STRUCTURE

Date: September 29, 2026
Status: ACCEPTED
Classification: INTERNAL
Owner: North Assembly
Related: NA-COR-001, root README.txt, DOCUMENT INVENTORY.txt

CONTEXT

Documentation previously lived in an unnumbered tree (the "Esp"
layout) mixed with placeholder files of no content.

PROBLEM

A documentation system needs stable areas, predictable ordering,
and a clear boundary between corporate, legal, product,
operational, public, internal and historical material.

DECISION

The repository uses eight numbered top-level sections:

  01 - CORPORATE
  02 - LEGAL
  03 - PRODUCTS
  04 - OPERATIONS
  05 - BRAND
  06 - PUBLIC
  07 - INTERNAL
  08 - ARCHIVE

Product directories use a secondary numeric order so the most
important products remain easy to find.

CONSEQUENCES

* The top-level structure is stable; new areas require an ADR.
* Every controlled document appears in DOCUMENT INVENTORY.txt.
* The old layout is preserved under 08 - ARCHIVE rather than
  deleted.

ALTERNATIVES CONSIDERED

* Topic-based folders without numbering — rejected: ordering
  becomes arbitrary.
* One folder per document — rejected: fragmentation.
