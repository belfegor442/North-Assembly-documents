ADR-0001
REBRAND FROM WEIRD STUFF TO NORTH ASSEMBLY

Date: September 29, 2026
Status: ACCEPTED
Classification: INTERNAL
Owner: North Assembly
Related: NA-COR-002, NA-COR-003, 05 - BRAND, 08 - ARCHIVE

CONTEXT

North Assembly previously operated publicly under the name
"Weird Stuff". Documentation, branding, and the public website
were built around that identity.

PROBLEM

The organization needed a single current identity across
documentation, products, branding, and public communication.

DECISION

North Assembly is the current organizational identity. Weird
Stuff is historical.

RATIONALE

The change is an evolution of identity and organization. It is
not evidence of any legal transformation, and no legal entity
change is implied or claimed.

CONSEQUENCES

* Active documentation uses "North Assembly".
* "Weird Stuff" appears only in historical or archive material,
  or where a document explicitly describes the name history.
* The WS logo asset is retained under 08 - ARCHIVE/Legacy Brand
  and is not a current brand asset.
* The public website still carries the previous identity in many
  pages; that is tracked as an external follow-up.

ALTERNATIVES CONSIDERED

* Keep both names in active use — rejected: creates permanent
  ambiguity about who is speaking.
* Remove all traces of the previous name — rejected: destroys
  history that documents products and decisions.
