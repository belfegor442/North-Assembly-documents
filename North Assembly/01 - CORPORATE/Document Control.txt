============================================================
1. PURPOSE
============================================================

This document defines how North Assembly identifies, classifies,
versions, and relates its controlled documentation.

Its purpose is not bureaucracy. Its purpose is to make it obvious,
at a glance and months later, what a document is, whether it is
current, who it is for, and what it replaces.

[MANDATORY]
Every controlled document must carry a document ID, title, status,
classification, version, owner, and last-modified date.

[MANDATORY]
A superseded document must not be presented or treated as the
current authoritative version.

============================================================
2. DOCUMENT IDENTIFICATION
============================================================

Every controlled document carries a stable identifier:

  NA-<AREA>-<NUMBER>

Area codes:

  NA-COR-xxx   Corporate
  NA-LGL-xxx   Legal
  NA-PRD-xxx   Products (with product code, e.g. NA-PRD-MON-001)
  NA-OPS-xxx   Operations
  NA-BRD-xxx   Brand
  NA-PUB-xxx   Public
  NA-INT-xxx   Internal
  NA-ARC-xxx   Archive
  ADR-xxxx     Architecture / organizational decision record

[MANDATORY]
A document ID never changes, even if the file is renamed or moved.

[MANDATORY]
A document ID is never reused for a different document.

[RECOMMENDED]
Files should be named so that the ID can be located quickly, but
the ID inside the document is authoritative.

============================================================
3. DOCUMENT STATUS
============================================================

STATUS describes the document's authority. Exactly one value:

DRAFT
Being written or substantially incomplete. Not authoritative.

REVIEW
Complete enough to be reviewed. Not yet approved.

APPROVED
Approved internally. This is an internal fact only. It does not
mean legally reviewed, signed, filed, or binding on anyone.

ACTIVE
Currently in force and applicable.

DEPRECATED
Replaced by a newer document. Still readable, no longer current.

ARCHIVED
Historical material retained under archive rules.

EXPERIMENTAL
Provisional. May be withdrawn or rewritten without notice.

[MANDATORY]
Exactly one STATUS value per document.

[MANDATORY]
The existence of a file in this repository does not make it an
active policy. Its STATUS does.

============================================================
4. CLASSIFICATION
============================================================

CLASSIFICATION describes who may read the document. It is never
stored in the STATUS field.

PUBLIC
Approved for distribution outside North Assembly.

INTERNAL
For North Assembly members and authorized collaborators.

RESTRICTED
For specifically authorized recipients only.

CONFIDENTIAL
Unauthorized disclosure would materially harm North Assembly,
its users, members, or projects.

RESERVED
Information intentionally withheld, whether private, undecided,
or otherwise restricted.

REPOSITORY VISIBILITY RULE (ADR-0006)

This repository is public. Therefore:

[MANDATORY]
RESTRICTED and CONFIDENTIAL material must not be stored in this
repository. It must be held in a location whose access controls
match its classification.

[MANDATORY]
A document stored here must not be labelled RESTRICTED or
CONFIDENTIAL. If a document needs that label, it is in the wrong
place.

[MANDATORY]
INTERNAL documents in this repository must not contain secrets,
credentials, personal contact data, private personnel records, or
infrastructure details that are not already public.

============================================================
5. SECONDARY ATTRIBUTES
============================================================

These describe completeness and review state. They are separate
from STATUS so that a document can be ACTIVE and still contain
pending items.

COMPLETENESS
  COMPLETE          No known pending content.
  PENDING ITEMS(n)  Contains explicit [PENDING] markers.

LEGAL REVIEW
  NOT REQUIRED      Internal or technical document.
  REQUIRED          Must be legally reviewed before publication.
  PASSED            Review completed and recorded.
  NOT PASSED        Review attempted and rejected.

[MANDATORY]
A legal document with LEGAL REVIEW: REQUIRED must not be
published or presented as effective terms.

============================================================
6. STANDARD HEADER
============================================================

Every controlled document begins with:

------------------------------------------------------------
NORTH ASSEMBLY
<TITLE>

DOCUMENT ID: NA-XXX-NNN
Version: X.Y
Status: <STATUS>
Classification: <CLASSIFICATION>
Completeness: COMPLETE
Legal Review: <NOT REQUIRED | REQUIRED | PASSED | NOT PASSED>
Owner: <role or "North Assembly">
Created: <date>
Last Modified: <date>
Review Date: <date>
Supersedes: <ID + version, or "—">
Superseded By: <ID + version, or "—">
Related: <ID list>
------------------------------------------------------------

[MANDATORY]
A header block must not contradict the body of the document.

[MANDATORY]
If a document also carries a trailing DOCUMENT CONTROL block,
that block must repeat the header's Status and Version exactly.
A document with two different status values is a contradiction
and must be corrected, not interpreted.

[MANDATORY]
STATUS and CLASSIFICATION are different fields. A value such as
INTERNAL is a classification and must never appear in the Status
field; a value such as DRAFT FOR LEGAL REVIEW is not a status and
must never appear either.

[RECOMMENDED]
A document with no applicable relationship uses "—" rather than
omitting the field.

INDEX FILES

Root README.txt, DOCUMENT INVENTORY.txt and section README.txt
files are navigation aids. They are unversioned: they carry at
least DOCUMENT ID, Status, Classification and Last Modified, and
may add Type, Owner, and Related for context.

============================================================
7. VERSIONING
============================================================

Versions are X.Y. The major number changes when the meaning of
the document changes; the minor number changes for corrections,
clarification, and header maintenance.

[MANDATORY]
A material change requires a new version.

[MANDATORY]
A correction that does not change meaning may increment only the
minor version.

[MANDATORY]
Reformatting, header migration, and typographical fixes do not by
themselves require a version change.

[MANDATORY]
The effective date or Last Modified date must be updated when a
new version becomes current.

[MANDATORY]
Changes to legal documents must not be represented as legally
effective until the applicable review and publication requirements
have been completed.

============================================================
8. CHANGE CONTROL
============================================================

[MANDATORY]
Important changes must be traceable through version control.

[MANDATORY]
A document must not be silently replaced while retaining an
obsolete version identifier.

[MANDATORY]
A material organizational, product, legal, or policy decision
must be recorded as an ADR in 01 - CORPORATE/Decisions, not only
as an edit to an existing document.

[RECOMMENDED]
Material documents should include a revision history when that
history helps explain their development.

============================================================
9. SUPERSESSION AND CROSS-REFERENCES
============================================================

[MANDATORY]
When a document replaces another, both sides record it:
Supersedes on the new document, Superseded By on the old one.

[MANDATORY]
Documents reference each other by document ID and section number,
not by file path or line number. Paths and line numbers rot.

[MANDATORY]
A fact must have exactly one source document. Other documents
reference it rather than restating it.

Primary sources of truth:

  People, roles, vacancies      -> NA-COR-002 Organization
  Recruitment and vacancies     -> NA-COR-004 Recruitment and Roles
  Document rules                -> NA-COR-001 (this document)
  Project list and classification -> 03 - PRODUCTS registry
  Legal identity and contacts   -> NA-LGL-002 Privacy Policy
  Status vocabulary             -> this document

============================================================
10. CONTRADICTION HANDLING
============================================================

Documentation rot usually appears as two documents disagreeing.

[MANDATORY]
When a contradiction is found, it is recorded in
01 - CORPORATE/Decisions/Consistency Log.txt with the documents
involved and its resolution status.

[MANDATORY]
A contradiction must not be resolved by silently picking one
value. If the evidence is unclear, mark it [REQUIRES CONFIRMATION].

[MANDATORY]
A change to a primary source of truth (section 9) must update its
dependent documents in the same commit.

============================================================
11. ARCHIVE
============================================================

Old documentation is not automatically useless. Historical
versions matter when investigating past decisions, understanding
a product's development, or establishing what was previously in
force.

[MANDATORY]
Superseded documents must be retained when historical
traceability is required.

[MANDATORY]
Archived documents must be clearly distinguishable from current
policy and must not be presented as authoritative.

[MANDATORY]
Archived content is preserved as-is. Provenance is recorded in
the archive index, not by editing archived files.

============================================================
12. RELATIONSHIP WITH OTHER RULES
============================================================

This document governs documentation only. It does not override:

* Applicable law.
* Binding contractual obligations.
* The Manual Corporativo (NA-COR-003) on organizational matters.

[MANDATORY]
Where this document conflicts with applicable law, the law takes
precedence and this document must be reviewed.
