PURPOSE

This section contains the documents that define how North Assembly
is organized and how controlled company documentation is managed.

DOCUMENTS

Document Control.txt (NA-COR-001) — v2.0, ACTIVE
Document identification, status vocabulary, classification,
versioning, supersession, and contradiction handling. Read this
before changing any other document.

Manual Corporativo.txt (NA-COR-003) — v2.0, ACTIVE
General corporate framework and operating principles.

Organization.txt (NA-COR-002) — v2.0, ACTIVE
Roles, holders, vacancies, and authority structure. Single source
of truth for people.

Recruitment and Roles.txt (NA-COR-004) — v1.0, ACTIVE
When a role is created, when it stays vacant, and the terminology
for working relationships.

Decisions/
ADR-0001 to ADR-0006, plus the Consistency Log tracking open
contradictions between documents.

USE THIS SECTION FOR

- Company structure and authority.
- Vacancies and recruitment rules.
- Controlled-document rules.
- Why a structural or organizational decision was made.
- Open contradictions between documents.

DO NOT USE THIS SECTION FOR

- Legal terms and policies: see 02 - LEGAL.
- Project list, ownership and delegation: see 03 - PRODUCTS.
- Operational procedures: see 04 - OPERATIONS.
- Private personnel records: never stored in this repository
  (ADR-0006).
