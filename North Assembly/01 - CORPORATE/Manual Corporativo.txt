1. PURPOSE
============================================================

This Manual is the main internal reference for how North Assembly is
organized and operated. It covers authority, responsibilities,
products, development, quality assurance, security, access,
communications, and general operations.

It does not replace applicable law, contracts, or specific legal
and operational documents.

[MANDATORY]
Everyone acting on behalf of North Assembly must follow the mandatory
provisions that apply to their role and responsibilities.

[MANDATORY]
Internal policies and procedures must remain consistent with this
Manual unless an approved change establishes otherwise.

============================================================
2. STATUS AND RESERVED INFORMATION
============================================================

North Assembly may operate publicly under its brand while some legal,
corporate, or organizational information remains private.

RESERVED information includes:

* Legal entity name
* Legal form
* Registered address
* Jurisdiction
* Tax identification information
* Registration information
* Legal representative information
* Private contact information
* Other restricted legal information

[MANDATORY]
Reserved information must not be disclosed publicly without proper
authorization.

[MANDATORY]
No one may invent, alter, or knowingly misrepresent reserved
corporate or legal information.

============================================================
3. NORMATIVE CLASSIFICATION
============================================================

[MANDATORY]
MANDATORY means the requirement must be followed.

[PROCEDURE]
PROCEDURE describes how an adopted requirement is carried out.

[STANDARD]
STANDARD identifies an adopted technical or operational requirement.

[RECOMMENDED]
RECOMMENDED describes a preferred practice. It is not mandatory
solely because it appears in this Manual.

[PENDING]
PENDING identifies a matter that has not yet been formally defined
or adopted.

[RESERVED]
RESERVED identifies information intentionally restricted from
publication.

5. CORPORATE AUTHORITY
============================================================

Current internal structure:

CEO
|
+-- Director
|
+-- Manager
|
+-- Specialized Teams

5.1 CEO

The CEO is the highest internal executive authority of North Assembly,
subject to applicable legal limitations.

[MANDATORY]
The CEO may establish organizational direction, approve policies,
assign responsibilities, create or reorganize teams, approve major
projects, resolve authority disputes, delegate responsibilities,
and take urgent measures when necessary.

[MANDATORY]
Delegating a responsibility does not automatically transfer the
delegator's overall authority.

5.2 DIRECTOR

The Director is the highest operational authority below the CEO.

[MANDATORY]
The Director coordinates operations, supervises assigned managers
and teams, implements approved decisions, escalates significant
issues, and coordinates work between departments.

5.3 MANAGER

The Manager coordinates assigned teams and operational work.

[MANDATORY]
The Manager must monitor assigned work, communicate relevant issues,
and escalate matters outside their authority.

5.4 SPECIALIZED TEAMS

North Assembly may organize work into functions such as:

* Development
* Design
* QA / Testing
* Product
* Infrastructure
* Security

The composition of these teams may change as the needs of North
Assembly change.

============================================================
6. CURRENT ORGANIZATION
============================================================

The current roles, their holders, and any vacant positions are
recorded exclusively in the Organization document (NA-COR-002).

[MANDATORY]
This Manual must not restate the roster. Where a name or vacancy
is needed, NA-COR-002 is the source.

[MANDATORY]
A person may exercise authority associated with a role only when
that role has actually been assigned to them.

[MANDATORY]
No one may knowingly present themselves as having authority above
the authority actually granted to them.

[MANDATORY]
An internal role title is an operational label. It does not imply
a registered corporate office or a legal representative status.

============================================================
7. INTERNAL CONDUCT
============================================================

People working with North Assembly are expected to act responsibly and
use company resources only within the limits of their role.

[MANDATORY]
North Assembly resources must not intentionally be used for:

* Unauthorized access.
* Credential theft.
* Fraud.
* Deliberate malware distribution.
* Impersonation.
* Unauthorized attacks against systems.
* Malicious manipulation of infrastructure.
* Illegal activities.

[MANDATORY]
Restricted or confidential information must not be shared without
authorization.

[MANDATORY]
No person may intentionally interfere with another member's work,
accounts, repositories, infrastructure, or property without
authorization.

============================================================
8. CONFLICTS OF INTEREST
============================================================

[MANDATORY]
Relevant conflicts of interest must be disclosed to the person
responsible for the affected decision or area.

[RECOMMENDED]
Important decisions affected by a conflict should receive an
additional review where practical.

[MANDATORY]
A known conflict must not be concealed when it could materially
affect North Assembly or one of its projects.

============================================================
9. ACCESS CONTROL
============================================================

Access is granted according to actual operational need.

[MANDATORY]
Permissions must be limited to what is reasonably necessary.

[MANDATORY]
Access must be removed when the associated need no longer exists.

[MANDATORY]
Passwords and authentication credentials must not be shared.

[MANDATORY]
Administrative access must receive stronger protection than ordinary
user access.

[RECOMMENDED]
Multi-factor authentication should be enabled for administrative
and critical accounts whenever available.

[RECOMMENDED]
Critical permissions should be reviewed periodically.

============================================================
10. INTERNAL INFORMATION
============================================================

PUBLIC
Information intended for public release.

INTERNAL
Information intended for members of North Assembly.

RESTRICTED
Information available only to specifically authorized people.

CONFIDENTIAL
Information whose unauthorized disclosure could cause significant
harm to North Assembly, its members, users, or projects.

[MANDATORY]
Restricted and confidential information may only be shared with
authorized recipients.

[MANDATORY]
Access to internal information does not automatically grant
permission to publish or redistribute it.

============================================================
11. PERSONNEL AND COLLABORATORS
============================================================

Role creation, recruitment, vacancies and the terminology used
for different working relationships are governed by the
Recruitment and Roles document (NA-COR-004).

[MANDATORY]
Anyone given access to internal resources must have an identified
function, responsibility, or justified operational need.

[MANDATORY]
Responsibilities must be communicated clearly.

[RECOMMENDED]
New members should be introduced to the tools, responsibilities,
communication channels, and rules relevant to their work.

[MANDATORY]
When a person leaves North Assembly or no longer needs access, the
relevant permissions must be revoked.

[RECOMMENDED]
Where practical, important knowledge should be transferred before a
person leaves.

============================================================
13. PRODUCT LIFECYCLE
============================================================

CONCEPT -> DEVELOPMENT -> TESTING -> RELEASE -> MAINTENANCE -> RETIREMENT

[MANDATORY]
A product must not knowingly be presented as stable while it is
internally identified as experimental unless that status is clearly
communicated.

[MANDATORY]
Critical changes should have a recovery or rollback mechanism when
technically practical.

[RECOMMENDED]
Major products should maintain basic lifecycle documentation.

============================================================
14. SOFTWARE DEVELOPMENT
============================================================

14.1 SOURCE CODE

[MANDATORY]
Source code must be stored in authorized repositories or systems.

[MANDATORY]
Passwords, private keys, API keys, tokens, and other secrets must
not intentionally be committed to public repositories.

[MANDATORY]
Important modifications must remain traceable through version
control.

14.2 CODE REVIEW

[RECOMMENDED]
Important changes should be reviewed by another qualified person
when team size permits.

[MANDATORY]
Security-critical modifications must receive appropriate review
before release whenever the available team structure allows it.

14.3 DEPENDENCIES

[MANDATORY]
Important external dependencies must be known to the responsible
development team.

[MANDATORY]
Third-party software must be used according to its applicable
license.

[MANDATORY]
Software with an identified security or licensing risk that is
incompatible with the product must not knowingly be introduced.

[RECOMMENDED]
Complex products should maintain a dependency inventory.

============================================================
15. QUALITY ASSURANCE AND TESTING
============================================================

[MANDATORY]
Stable releases must receive reasonable testing appropriate to
their functionality and risk.

[MANDATORY]
Known critical defects affecting essential operation, security, or
data integrity must be evaluated before a stable release.

[MANDATORY]
Important bugs must contain enough information to understand their
impact and, when possible, reproduce the issue.

[RECOMMENDED]
Major changes should receive regression testing.

[RECOMMENDED]
Important fixes should retain evidence showing that the fix was
tested.

============================================================
16. RELEASE MANAGEMENT
============================================================

[MANDATORY]
Every public release must have an identifiable version.

[MANDATORY]
Published versions must remain distinguishable from development
versions.

[MANDATORY]
A release must not intentionally hide material differences from the
version it claims to be.

[RECOMMENDED]
Significant releases should include release notes.

[MANDATORY]
Automatic updates must use reasonable mechanisms to protect the
integrity and authenticity of distributed update packages.

[RECOMMENDED]
Artifact signing and integrity verification should be used for
important products where technically possible.

============================================================
17. SECURITY
============================================================

Security is a shared responsibility across North Assembly.

[MANDATORY]
Known significant vulnerabilities must be assessed and, where
appropriate, corrected.

[MANDATORY]
Credentials suspected of compromise must be revoked, rotated, or
otherwise secured as soon as reasonably possible.

[MANDATORY]
Security incidents must be reported to the appropriate responsible
person.

[MANDATORY]
Security controls must not intentionally be disabled without
authorization.

[RECOMMENDED]
Critical systems should use multi-factor authentication, logging,
access review, and backup controls where available.

============================================================
18. SECURITY INCIDENTS
============================================================

Security incidents may affect confidentiality, integrity,
availability, authenticity, user security, or North Assembly systems.

[MANDATORY]
Incidents must be reported as soon as reasonably possible.

General response order:
1. Contain
2. Protect
3. Recover
4. Investigate
5. Correct
6. Prevent recurrence

[RECOMMENDED]
Significant incidents should receive a post-incident review.

21. PRIVACY
============================================================

[MANDATORY]
Personal data must be processed according to applicable law.

[MANDATORY]
Only data reasonably necessary for defined purposes should be
collected.

[MANDATORY]
Personal data must be protected using appropriate security
measures.

[MANDATORY]

============================================================
22. COOKIES AND TRACKING
============================================================

[MANDATORY]
Cookies and similar technologies must be handled according to
applicable legal requirements.

[MANDATORY]
Where consent is legally required, an appropriate consent mechanism
must be used.

[MANDATORY]
Users must be able to manage or withdraw consent where legally
required.

Detailed implementation requirements are defined in the Cookie
Policy.

============================================================
23. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
============================================================

[MANDATORY]
North Assembly must protect intellectual property that it owns or is
legally entitled to use.

[MANDATORY]
Third-party material must not be incorporated without sufficient
rights or authorization.

[MANDATORY]
Third-party software licenses must be respected.

[MANDATORY]
North Assembly branding must not be used in a way that falsely implies
endorsement, authorization, partnership, or official representation.

Detailed licensing requirements are defined in the Intellectual
Property Policy.

============================================================
24. CUSTOM APPLICATIONS
============================================================

North Assembly may accept requests to create custom applications,
software, or digital projects.

[MANDATORY]
A request does not automatically create an obligation to develop the
application.

[MANDATORY]
Every request may be evaluated before acceptance.

[MANDATORY]
A request may be rejected because of legal, security, technical,
resource, requirements, project, policy, or other legitimate
operational concerns.

[MANDATORY]
An accepted project must have a defined purpose, scope, responsible
person, and general delivery method.

[PENDING]
Detailed intellectual property terms for custom applications.
[PENDING]
Detailed commercial terms for custom applications.

============================================================
25. COMMUNICATIONS
============================================================

Authorized channels may include official email, Discord, authorized
development systems, and other explicitly approved services.

[MANDATORY]
Sensitive internal matters must use appropriate authorized
channels.

[MANDATORY]
Confidential internal information must not be posted publicly.

[MANDATORY]
Only authorized persons may issue communications that reasonably
appear to represent the official position of North Assembly.

============================================================
26. PUBLIC COMMUNICATION
============================================================

Public communication is separate from internal corporate
documentation.

[MANDATORY]
Public announcements must not disclose reserved information.

[MANDATORY]
Public statements must not knowingly contain materially false
claims about North Assembly, its products, services, or capabilities.

[MANDATORY]
Internal policies must not be presented as user-facing legal terms
unless formally prepared for that purpose.

[RECOMMENDED]
Public announcements should focus on information relevant to users
and the community rather than internal administrative details.

============================================================
27. SUPPORT
============================================================

[MANDATORY]
North Assembly must clearly identify the support channels it actually
provides.

[MANDATORY]
North Assembly must not promise service levels, response times,
availability, or continuous support that have not been formally
adopted.

[RECOMMENDED]
Support requests should be classified by impact and urgency.

============================================================
28. CONFLICT RESOLUTION
============================================================

Internal responsibility conflicts should normally follow:

Responsible Person -> Manager -> Director -> CEO

[MANDATORY]
A higher authority may resolve an internal conflict when the parties
cannot reach a compatible resolution.

[MANDATORY]
A lower-level decision must not knowingly override a valid decision
from a higher authority without authorization.

============================================================
29. EXCEPTIONS
============================================================

[MANDATORY]
An exception to a mandatory internal rule must be authorized by a
person with sufficient authority.

[MANDATORY]
A temporary exception does not, by itself, change the permanent rule.

[RECOMMENDED]
Significant exceptions should be recorded.
