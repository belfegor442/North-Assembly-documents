============================================================
1. PURPOSE
============================================================

This document defines when North Assembly creates a role, when it
fills one, and when it deliberately leaves one vacant.

It exists because North Assembly previously experienced a mismatch
between organizational positions and actual productive work: roles
existed that were not backed by assigned, completed work.

The rule below prevents that from recurring.

============================================================
2. THE RECRUITMENT PIPELINE
============================================================

A position is created and filled only in this order:

  REAL NEED
     ->
  IDENTIFIED WORK
     ->
  PROJECT OR RESPONSIBILITY
     ->
  ROLE REQUIREMENT
     ->
  PERSON
     ->
  ASSIGNMENT
     ->
  DELIVERABLE

[MANDATORY]
A role may only be created when the preceding step is documented.

[MANDATORY]
The work backing a role must be identifiable in the project
registry (03 - PRODUCTS/README.txt) or in a corporate document
before the role is filled.

[MANDATORY]
An assignment must name what the person will deliver.

============================================================
3. PROHIBITED PATTERN
============================================================

The following order is not permitted:

  PERSON
     ->
  "Find something for them to do"

[MANDATORY]
North Assembly must not create artificial work in order to justify
a Developer, Designer, or other position.

[MANDATORY]
North Assembly must not assign a person to a role that has no
identified work behind it.

[MANDATORY]
A role must not be kept open as decoration. If no work justifies
it, it stays VACANT in NA-COR-002 with the reason recorded.

============================================================
4. DELEGATION AND SUITABILITY
============================================================

Not every project can be handed to a new person.

[MANDATORY]
Projects involving architecture, core engineering, internal
infrastructure, systems programming, internal tooling,
organization-specific knowledge, or highly specialized technical
decisions must be assessed as non-delegable or limited-delegable
before recruitment is considered on their basis.

[MANDATORY]
The delegation level recorded in the project registry is the
input to this assessment. Recruitment must not assume a project
is delegable when its registry entry says otherwise.

[RECOMMENDED]
Where design work is currently limited, that fact should be
recorded rather than solved by creating a position.

============================================================
5. WHEN A ROLE IS VACANT
============================================================

A vacant role is a normal state.

[MANDATORY]
Vacancies are recorded in NA-COR-002 with the reason they are
unfilled.

[MANDATORY]
A vacant role grants no access, no authority, and no ability to
act for North Assembly.

[MANDATORY]
Vacancies must not be described as organizational failure in any
document or public communication.

============================================================
6. WHEN A ROLE IS FILLED
============================================================

[MANDATORY]
A person must have an identified function, responsibility, or
justified operational need before receiving access to internal
resources.

[MANDATORY]
Responsibilities must be communicated clearly to the person
filling the role.

[MANDATORY]
Access must be granted according to the actual role and need,
never by default.

[RECOMMENDED]
New members should be introduced to the tools, responsibilities,
communication channels, and rules relevant to their work.

============================================================
7. TERMINOLOGY
============================================================

The following words are not synonyms and must not be used
interchangeably:

  employee        person with an employment relationship
  collaborator    person contributing without that relationship
  volunteer       person contributing without expectation of pay
  contractor      person engaged under a service contract
  supplier        external provider of goods or services
  partner         party in a defined cooperation arrangement
  community member person present in a communication channel
  administrator  person holding access rights
  role holder     person recorded in NA-COR-002

[MANDATORY]
Where the exact relationship is unknown, use the neutral term
"collaborator" or "member of the project".

[MANDATORY]
An internal role title must never be presented as a legal or
corporate office.

============================================================
8. DEPARTURES
============================================================

[MANDATORY]
When a person leaves a role or leaves North Assembly, applicable
access must be revoked.

[MANDATORY]
When a role becomes vacant, its holder field in NA-COR-002 must
be updated in the same change cycle.

[RECOMMENDED]
Important work should be documented well enough for
responsibilities to be transferred.
