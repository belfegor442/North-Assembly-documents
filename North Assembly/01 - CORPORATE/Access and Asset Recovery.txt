JOINER

Access only after role assignment and need confirmation.

MOVER

Review old permissions before granting new ones.

LEAVER

1. Revoke privileged access.
2. Rotate credentials/secrets when appropriate.
3. Transfer repositories/domains/services.
4. Recover organization-owned equipment.
5. Review active sessions/tokens.
6. Update asset and role registers.
7. Preserve relevant records.

CRITICAL RECOVERY

Each critical system must have an organization-controlled owner, primary custodian, backup custodian, MFA recovery, vendor recovery route, and documented transfer procedure.

NO SINGLE POINT OF HUMAN CONTROL

No critical asset should become unrecoverable merely because one person is unavailable or loses access.
