============================================================
1. PURPOSE
============================================================

This document records how North Assembly is currently organized,
who holds each role, which roles are vacant, and how assignments
change.

It is the single source of truth for people and roles. No other
document may enumerate personnel. Other documents reference this
one.

It contains operational role information only. It contains no
private personnel data: no contact details, no employment or
contract records, no performance or disciplinary information, and
no record about anyone who is not a current member.

[MANDATORY]
Statements about work, output or conduct must not be attributed to
an identifiable individual anywhere in this repository. Former
members are referenced by role only (ADR-0007).

============================================================
2. AUTHORITY STRUCTURE
============================================================

CEO
|
+-- Director
|
+-- Manager
|
+-- Project roles (assigned per project, not per person)

The structure above is the internal chain of authority. Individual
projects may use different working arrangements without changing
the formal structure unless those arrangements are formally
assigned.

[MANDATORY]
Authority exists only within the role formally assigned to a
person.

[MANDATORY]
Delegating a task or responsibility does not transfer the
delegator's overall authority to the person receiving it.

[MANDATORY]
An internal role is an operational label. It does not imply a
registered corporate office, a legal representative status, or
any employment or commercial relationship under applicable law.

============================================================
4. VACANT POSITIONS
============================================================

A vacant position is a deliberate state, not a defect.

[MANDATORY]
A position exists because identified work requires it. It does
not exist to complete an organizational chart.

[MANDATORY]
A vacant position must be recorded with its state and the reason
it is unfilled, never silently omitted.

Current vacancies:

  Developer — VACANT
  Filled: no
  Reason: no currently identified project work meets the criteria
          in NA-COR-004 for creating this position.

  Designer — VACANT
  Filled: no
  Reason: no currently identified design work meets the criteria
          in NA-COR-004 for creating this position.

[MANDATORY]
A vacancy must not be described as an organizational failure.

[MANDATORY]
A vacant position grants no access, no authority, and no
representation right to anyone.

============================================================
5. TEAMS AND FUNCTIONS
============================================================

North Assembly may organize work into functions such as:

* Development
* Design
* QA / Testing
* Product
* Infrastructure
* Security
* Documentation

These are functions, not titles. A function may be performed by
the same person holding another role.

[MANDATORY]
A function name must not be recorded as a person's role unless
the assignment has actually been made.

============================================================
6. PROJECT ROLES
============================================================

Projects may need owners beyond the corporate chain. Project
ownership is recorded in the project registry
(03 - PRODUCTS/README.txt), not here.

[MANDATORY]
Project ownership is assigned per project and does not change the
corporate authority structure.

[MANDATORY]
Some projects — architecture, core engineering, internal
infrastructure, systems programming, organization-specific
tooling — are not automatically suitable for delegation. Their
delegation level is recorded in the registry.

============================================================
7. ROLE CHANGES
============================================================

Organizational changes must leave a clear record of who is
responsible for what. This matters most when a role change also
changes access to repositories, systems, projects, or internal
information.

[MANDATORY]
Assignments, removals, suspensions, and transfers must be
authorized by the appropriate authority.

[MANDATORY]
Access permissions must reflect the person's current role and
actual operational need.

[MANDATORY]
When a role changes, obsolete permissions must be reviewed and
removed.

[MANDATORY]
When a role becomes vacant, the associated access must be
reviewed and removed until the role is filled again.

============================================================
8. SINGLE SOURCE OF TRUTH
============================================================

[MANDATORY]
This document is the only place where the list of holders and
vacancies is recorded.

[MANDATORY]
The Manual Corporativo (NA-COR-003) must reference this document
rather than restating the roster.

[MANDATORY]
Public material must not contradict this document. When this
document changes, public people/role pages must be updated in the
same change cycle.
