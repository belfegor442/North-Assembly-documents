PURPOSE

This directory contains approved public announcements and the
standards used for publishing them. It is intended for information
that is ready to be shared publicly, not for internal drafts or
working material.

CURRENT STATE

STRUCTURE ONLY. No announcement has been issued through this
directory.

[MANDATORY]
The absence of announcements must not be presented as if
announcements existed elsewhere as approved records.

RULES

[MANDATORY]
Only approved public information may be published through this directory.

[MANDATORY]
Internal, restricted, confidential, or reserved information must not be published here.

[MANDATORY]
Material product status changes must be communicated accurately,
and must match the project registry (03 - PRODUCTS/README.txt).

[MANDATORY]
An announcement must not describe a legal document as effective
while that document is still DRAFT.

[RECOMMENDED]
Announcements should make the practical change clear rather than relying on vague wording.

[RECOMMENDED]
Each announcement should record its date, the affected product or
area, and the document or decision it refers to.

END OF DOCUMENT
