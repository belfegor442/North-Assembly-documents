PURPOSE

These standards govern official North Assembly website content. The website should give visitors an accurate picture of the company, its products, and their current status.

1. CONTENT

[MANDATORY]
Published information must accurately represent North Assembly, its products, and their status.

[MANDATORY]
Public legal documents must not contain unresolved placeholders when they are presented as effective legal documents.

[MANDATORY]
Internal, restricted, confidential, and reserved information must not be published.

2. PRODUCT STATUS

Product pages must distinguish active, development, beta, experimental, suspended, and retired products whenever those distinctions are relevant.

3. LEGAL CONTENT

[MANDATORY]
Terms, privacy, cookie, acceptable-use, intellectual-property, security, and custom-application documents must remain consistent with their approved versions.

[MANDATORY]
Legal documents whose Publication Status is not CONFIRMED must not be presented on the website as effective or in force. Draft documents may only be published if they are clearly marked DRAFT and contain no unresolved placeholders that would mislead a User.

[MANDATORY]
This document requires legal review before its rules are relied on to publish legal content (Legal Review: REQUIRED, 2026-10-01, see Consistency Log C-019).

4. ACCESSIBILITY AND QUALITY

[RECOMMENDED]
Pages should be readable, navigable, responsive, and accessible to users with different devices and abilities.

[PENDING]
Formal accessibility baseline.
[PENDING]
Formal content review schedule.
