NORTH ASSEMBLY
PUBLIC DISCORD COMPANY UPDATE STANDARD

Version: 1.1
Status: IN FORCE
Classification: PUBLIC

PURPOSE

This document defines the baseline for official North Assembly company updates published through Discord. The aim is to keep public updates clear, accurate, and safe to publish.

1. REQUIREMENTS

[MANDATORY]
Official updates must be truthful and attributable to North Assembly.

[MANDATORY]
Confidential or restricted internal information must not be included.

[MANDATORY]
Product withdrawals, retirements, major releases, and significant organizational changes must be described accurately.

[RECOMMENDED]
Updates should identify the affected product or area, its current status, the effective date when relevant, and the practical consequence for users or team members.

2. SECURITY

[MANDATORY]
Credentials, tokens, private infrastructure information, and other secrets must never be published.

[RECOMMENDED]
When an update concerns a security or operational incident, it should communicate what users need to know without exposing information that could create additional risk.

END OF DOCUMENT
