NORTH ASSEMBLY
DOCUMENT CONTROL

Version: 1.1
Status: IN FORCE
Effective Date: September 12, 2026
Owner: North Assembly
Classification: INTERNAL

============================================================
1. PURPOSE
============================================================

This document explains how North Assembly keeps its controlled documentation consistent, current, and traceable.

The purpose is not to add unnecessary bureaucracy. It is to make it clear which document is current, what a document means, and when a change became effective.

[MANDATORY]
A controlled document must have an identifiable title, version, status, owner, classification, and effective date when an effective date is applicable.

[MANDATORY]
A superseded document must not be presented or treated as the current authoritative version.

============================================================
2. DOCUMENT STATUS
============================================================

IN FORCE
The document is currently approved and applicable.

DRAFT FOR REVIEW
The document is being prepared or reviewed. It is not yet authoritative.

PENDING
The document structure exists, but required information, decisions, or approval are still missing.

RETIRED
The document is no longer applicable but is being retained for reference.

ARCHIVED
The document is historical or superseded material retained under archive controls.

A document's status is part of its meaning. The existence of a file in the repository does not, by itself, make that file an active policy.

============================================================
3. VERSIONING
============================================================

Versions exist to make meaningful changes identifiable. They are not intended to be changed merely because a file was opened or reformatted.

[MANDATORY]
A material change requires a new document version.

[MANDATORY]
The effective date must be updated when a new version enters into force.

[MANDATORY]
Changes to legal documents must not be represented as legally effective until the applicable review and publication requirements have been completed.

============================================================
4. CLASSIFICATION
============================================================

PUBLIC
Information approved for public distribution.

INTERNAL
Information intended for North Assembly personnel and authorized collaborators.

RESTRICTED
Information available only to specifically authorized recipients.

CONFIDENTIAL
Information whose unauthorized disclosure could materially harm North Assembly, its users, members, or projects.

RESERVED
Information intentionally withheld from publication, whether because it is private, undecided, or otherwise restricted.

============================================================
5. CHANGE CONTROL
============================================================

Documentation should be understandable not only when it is written, but also when someone needs to understand it months later.

[MANDATORY]
Important changes must be traceable through version control.

[MANDATORY]
A document must not be silently replaced while retaining an obsolete version identifier.

[RECOMMENDED]
Material documents should include a revision history when the history is useful for understanding their development.

============================================================
6. ARCHIVE
============================================================

Old documentation is not automatically useless. Historical versions can be important when investigating past decisions, understanding a product's development, or establishing what rules were previously in force.

[MANDATORY]
Superseded documents must be retained when historical traceability is required.

[MANDATORY]
Archived documents must be clearly distinguishable from current policy and must not be presented as authoritative.

============================================================
END OF DOCUMENT
