============================================================
1. PURPOSE
============================================================

This Policy sets out the general rules for intellectual property
used, created, owned, licensed, distributed, or otherwise handled by
North Assembly.

It covers software, source code, compiled software, designs,
graphics, branding, documentation, text, audiovisual material,
digital assets, websites, product names, repositories, and other
relevant intellectual property.

============================================================
2. SCOPE
============================================================

This Policy applies to:

* North Assembly products.
* Internal projects.
* Public releases.
* Source-code repositories.
* Designs and visual assets.
* Documentation.
* Websites.
* Branding.
* Custom applications.
* Third-party software and assets.
* User-submitted material where applicable.

[MANDATORY]
Anyone acting on behalf of North Assembly must follow the intellectual
property requirements that apply to their work.

============================================================
3. OWNERSHIP
============================================================

Intellectual property may belong to:

* North Assembly.
* Individual creators.
* Contractors or collaborators.
* Third-party licensors.
* Users.
* Other rights holders.

[MANDATORY]
Access to intellectual property does not, by itself, create an
ownership right.

[MANDATORY]
Ownership must be determined according to applicable law, contracts,
licenses, and the circumstances in which the work was created.

============================================================
4. NORTH ASSEMBLY INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
============================================================

North Assembly may own or control rights in:

* Software and source code.
* Compiled binaries.
* Product names.
* Logos and brand assets.
* User interfaces.
* Graphics.
* Documentation.
* Websites.
* Original text.
* Original audiovisual content.
* Other original works.

The complete list of registered or formally protected North Assembly
intellectual property is not published through this Policy.

============================================================
5. PRODUCT INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
============================================================

The following North Assembly products may contain intellectual property
belonging to North Assembly or other rights holders:

* Monix
* Datalog
* CryptCast
* DraftPad
* Telenet

[MANDATORY]
Listing a product here does not mean that every component of that
product is exclusively owned by North Assembly.

Third-party components remain subject to their own licenses.

============================================================
6. SOURCE CODE
============================================================

[MANDATORY]
Source code belonging to North Assembly must not be reproduced,
redistributed, sold, publicly disclosed, or incorporated into another
project in a way that violates applicable rights, agreements, or
licenses.

[MANDATORY]
Open-source repositories must be handled according to the license
that applies to the relevant repository or component.

============================================================
7. SOFTWARE LICENSES
============================================================

Software distributed by North Assembly may be subject to product-
specific license terms.

[MANDATORY]
Users must comply with the license that applies to the software they
use.

Before a public product is released, its applicable license should be
identified.

Possible models include:

* Proprietary.
* Open source.
* Free software.
* Custom license.
* Third-party licensed software.

The actual model must be determined for each product.

============================================================
8. OPEN SOURCE
============================================================

North Assembly may use, modify, distribute, or contribute to
open-source software.

[MANDATORY]
Applicable open-source license requirements must be respected. These
may include:

* Attribution.
* Copyright notices.
* License notices.
* Redistribution requirements.
* Source-code availability requirements.
* Modification notices.
* Other license conditions.

[MANDATORY]
Developers must not intentionally remove or bypass an applicable
open-source license requirement.

[RECOMMENDED]
Projects should keep a record of important open-source dependencies
and their licenses.

============================================================
9. THIRD-PARTY ASSETS
============================================================

Third-party assets may include:

* Fonts.
* Images.
* Icons.
* Sounds.
* Music.
* Libraries.
* Frameworks.
* Models.
* Code.
* Documentation.
* Templates.
* Other digital resources.

[MANDATORY]
Third-party assets may only be used when North Assembly has a sufficient
legal right or license to use them.

[MANDATORY]
For important third-party assets, the source and applicable license
should be known before distribution.

============================================================
10. COPYRIGHT
============================================================

Copyright may protect original works used or created by North Assembly,
subject to applicable law.

[MANDATORY]
Copyright notices, attribution statements, and license requirements
must be preserved when required.

[MANDATORY]
No person may falsely claim authorship of work created by someone
else.

[MANDATORY]
Third-party works remain subject to their creators' rights unless
those rights have been validly transferred, licensed, or otherwise
obtained.

============================================================
11. TRADEMARKS AND BRANDING
============================================================

North Assembly may use company names, product names, logos, visual
identities, and other branding elements.

[MANDATORY]
North Assembly branding must not knowingly be used in a way that falsely
suggests:

* Official endorsement.
* Official representation.
* Ownership where none exists.
* Partnership where none exists.
* Sponsorship where none exists.

[MANDATORY]
Official changes to company branding require authorization.

============================================================
12. INTERNAL CREATIONS
============================================================

When a person creates code, graphics, documentation, or other work
for North Assembly:

[MANDATORY]
Ownership and usage rights must be determined according to applicable
agreements and law.

[MANDATORY]
Participation in a project does not automatically transfer every
legal right to North Assembly.

Standard agreements for ownership and assignment remain to be
defined where necessary.

============================================================
13. COLLABORATORS AND CONTRACTORS
============================================================

When an external collaborator creates material for North Assembly:

[MANDATORY]
The relevant ownership or licensing rights should be established
through an appropriate agreement where necessary.

[MANDATORY]
North Assembly must not claim rights that it has not legally obtained.

============================================================
14. CUSTOM APPLICATIONS
============================================================

Custom applications may involve contributions from North Assembly,
Users, clients, or other parties.

[MANDATORY]
Ownership and licensing of custom work must be defined before the
project is completed where the issue is legally relevant.

The Custom Applications Policy provides additional rules for these
projects.

============================================================
15. USER CONTENT
============================================================

Users may provide content to North Assembly services.

[MANDATORY]
Users must not submit material they do not have the right to use
when doing so would violate applicable law or another person's
rights.

[MANDATORY]
North Assembly must not claim ownership of User content merely because
the User submitted it, unless an applicable agreement expressly
provides otherwise.

Any license granted by Users to North Assembly must be defined by the
relevant service or agreement.

============================================================
16. LICENSE GRANTS
============================================================

When North Assembly grants a User a license to software or other
intellectual property:

[MANDATORY]
The scope of the license is determined by the applicable license
terms.

Possible characteristics include:

* Personal use.
* Commercial use.
* Modification.
* Distribution.
* Sublicensing.
* Term.
* Territory.
* Platform restrictions.

Product-specific license terms remain to be defined where needed.

============================================================
17. UNAUTHORIZED COPYING
============================================================

[MANDATORY]
Users and members must not knowingly reproduce or distribute
protected North Assembly material beyond the permissions granted by the
applicable license or law.

This may include:

* Source code.
* Proprietary binaries.
* Restricted assets.
* Internal documentation.
* Protected graphics.
* Proprietary product material.

============================================================
18. REVERSE ENGINEERING
============================================================

The final rules concerning reverse engineering, decompilation,
disassembly, and modification must be defined for each product and
reviewed against applicable law.

[MANDATORY]
Nothing in this Policy is intended to restrict rights that cannot
legally be restricted.

============================================================
19. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY INFRINGEMENT
============================================================

If North Assembly receives a credible report that code, content,
design, or another asset may infringe third-party rights:

[MANDATORY]
The relevant material must be reviewed.

Appropriate measures may be considered where legally and
operationally justified, including:

* Investigation.
* Temporary removal.
* Replacement.
* License verification.
* Contact with the rights holder.
* Attribution correction.
* Legal review.

Domains and official digital identifiers may form part of Weird

[MANDATORY]

[MANDATORY]
Credentials for important domains and digital identity systems must

============================================================
24. PUBLICATION
============================================================

[MANDATORY]
Before publishing intellectual property belonging to another party,
North Assembly must verify that publication is permitted.

[MANDATORY]
Required attribution and license notices must be included where
applicable.

[RECOMMENDED]
Major publications should receive an appropriate review before
release.

============================================================
25. ENFORCEMENT
============================================================

[MANDATORY]
Violations of this Policy may result in internal action appropriate

Possible measures include:

* Removal of unauthorized material.
* Access restriction.
* Project reassignment.
* Suspension of access.
* Internal disciplinary action where applicable.
* Legal review.
* Other measures permitted by law.

28. LEGAL LIMITATION
============================================================

Nothing in this Policy is intended to:

* Transfer ownership merely by declaration.
* Override applicable law.
* Remove non-waivable legal rights.
* Create ownership where no legal basis exists.
* Replace a necessary intellectual-property agreement.

[MANDATORY]
Where ownership or licensing requires a formal agreement, the
appropriate agreement must be used.
